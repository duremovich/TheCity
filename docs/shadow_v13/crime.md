3. **A stakeout with no target arc.** Mechanism: a taken contract gets a deadline and an expiry that posts `ContractLost` and a debt; the stakeout line names its mark and a result (spotted, not home, guarded).
4. **Jail's inside is still needs rows.** Two pimps and a fixer-leader spend 5-6 days at 16 h/day with MetInJail only. Mechanism: a cellmate's debt, a crew's visit, a meal ration, an exercise-yard brawl as an event line.
