# Shadow V5: labour critic

Group: worker, worker_friday, night_shift, fabber, loader, builder, foreman, courier, cabbie, day_labourer. 57 diaries, all ten archetypes in all three windows (day_labourer 2 + 2 + 1). Shorthand: s43 = k2_s43_d26, s44 = k2_s44_d90, k20 = k20_s42_d20. All picks are Full (pinned). Method: header, summary, scripted counts of `served` memories, hang-out lines, goal lines, then a day read where a story showed.

## Verdict in one paragraph
The counterpart is half alive: 43 of 57 diaries carry `served <name>` memories and 24 show "a regular"; the foreman hires; the loader lifts and the owner's pilfer report fires; the courier's PreconditionLost is gone. The two headline street mechanisms are not visible: no cabbie drives a fare in any of six diaries, and no courier diary says "parcel". The household evening is nearly absent: 49 of 53 married picks never name the spouse at a hang-out (V4: 14 of 17 never met). One new bug: the Visit goal beats Work and cannot plan.

## Bugs

1. **The Visit goal wins at 1.08 over Work and then cannot plan: 82 failures in 8 diaries.** `GOAL Visit (was Work): score 1.080; runners-up: Work 0.985 ... a visit due 1.00->1.00, the reason 0.60->0.80`, then `goal Visit cooled until +2h (could not plan or plan failed twice)` and Work a minute later (`s43/courier_2279 d28 10:29`, 12:29 and 14 more times that week). Per diary: worker_11442 17, courier_2279 16, worker_886 11, builder_1362 11, worker_friday_1236 10, night_shift_1997 9, night_shift_1543 8. Only 2 of 84 wins produced a plan, and both are 195-minute walks to the spouse (`s44/night_shift_1997 d93 21:02` GoTo(Victim) across Civic). Each win interrupts the shift (`plan interrupted: Work -> Visit`). Likely cause: the phase-3 spouse/kin visit (§ 3.5) targets a Home with no usable route, the cooldown is 2 h and the flat bonus puts it above Work. Full-tier, 8 of 57 diaries.

2. **No ride is booked to a pinned cab driver.** 0 fares in 6 of 6 cabbie diaries (two picks per window, 58-59 h of Work each on 4 of them). The stand-by body works (Rest at the Garage), the rider side works (cabbie_1578 pays Fare flows; fare evasion is witnessed), so the Garage's off-screen cab is taking every ride. § 14 phase 4a gives the ride to a standing-by driver who can reach the rider within `cab_wait_ticks`, else the Garage's off-screen cab (`cab_rides_unmanned`). Check the counter first: a 2k district whose rider homes are all out of `cab_wait_ticks` of the Garage at cab speed (cabbie_1578's Garage is in Civic, his Block in Mid West) would give exactly this. 6 of 6 diaries, Full-tier.

3. **Couriers 64-73 still work 57-62 h a week; the age band does not touch incumbents.** Courier `max_age` is 60, so the band only gates hires. Retirement needs 60 days of costs in household coins or an adult child to take the elder in; the frailty exit is `work_mult < 0.6`, and with `frail_from_years` 60 and `frail_slope` 0.03 work_mult is 0.61 at 73, so a poor labourer is let go at about 74. No labour diary has a retirement event (one office worker does: `k20/office_worker_24433 d21`). Over 60: 9 of 57 (V4: 15 of 50): couriers 73, 71, 68, 67, 64; Sanitation 71 (worker_friday_1026), Night Stocker 65, Fab Tech 66 (fabber_919), foreman 60. Partly design (no pension), partly numbers: see gate item.

4. **The foreman premium is invisible in k20.** foreman_6844 and foreman_10662 are paid +3-4c, the same as builder_22146 +3-4c; in s43 and s44 the premium shows (+5-6 vs +4). The k20 foreman hires 24-48 day labourers, so the "crew of two or more" is met. Likely rounding of the 1.25 premium on a 6/day wage after the pay-rate cut. Full-tier, 2 of 4 foreman diaries; weak (a few coins).

5. **A married labourer's evening is not with the spouse.** 53 of 57 diaries have a [Spouse] edge; 4 of 53 (7 %) name the spouse at any hang-out in the week (worker_friday_16743 14 of 21, cabbie_1858 5 of 19, cabbie_25208 4 of 26, cabbie_2325 3 of 25). V4: 14 of 17 never met; now 49 of 53 never name the spouse at a hang-out. 338 of 966 hang-outs (35 %) are at the pick's own Block but mostly with "1 stranger(s) [0 known]" (night_shift_1543: 43 of 48 at home, spouse Flux Osgood#893 elsewhere). Likely cause: marriage edges are seeded across Homes, so the household-evening rule (§ 3.1) has no household to gather. Full-tier, 49 of 53 married diaries. Caveat: this counts hang-out lines only; the spouse could be at home during an action that logs no hang-out.

6. **`served <name>` has no kind.** The same memory line is a landlord's rent (`s44/cabbie_1858 d93 09:33` Raven Delacroix, the rent), a co-worker, a parcel recipient, a customer, a client. `k20/cabbie_17783` has `served Tomas Mbeki` at 11:24 on six days running. It is why the cab, parcel and the household gates cannot be read from the text. Diary-side readability only; weak.
