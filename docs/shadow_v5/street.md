Spec § 2.3 expects a named client near the pitch in the evening (`vice::street_hour`'s `street_payer`, `client_tiles`). Full-tier evidence; misses the phase 4a "client nearby" bullet.
3. **The Camp's children are never visible beyond the 09:00 count** (6 of 6 wardens). The counted names are tagged "a regular" (the vendor label) with valence -0.10. Either the Camp holds no children in these windows (then the headcount lines are mislabelled regulars) or the daily visit, buy-out and break-out rolls never reach the warden's feed. The diary cannot tell which; the counters (`camp_visits`, `camp_buyouts`, `camp_breakouts`, `camp_headcounts` in the Jobs2 CSV for these windows) can. Full-tier evidence.

A foreman pick feeds the existing Housed rule and a person with 3-8c is indoors by the next midnight (k2b/839 is housed twice in one midnight, "moved into Block#278" then "moved nearer work, into Block#92", then quits "(unpaid)" and is re-hired d94).
