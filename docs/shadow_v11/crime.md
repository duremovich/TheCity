# Shadow V11: the crime group (critic: crime)

Window: 100k island, seed 42, d21-27. Seventeen diaries: forger 102600 and 30371, fixer 22056 and 23959, gun 38550 and 40332, buyer 76527 and 82590, pimp 25262 and 47630, arms_dealer 65421, black_doc 109021 and 116826, jailed 16869 and 75054, dodger 103790 and 29048. Cites are `<arch>_<index> dDD HH:MM`. Picks differ from V10 except fixer_23959, so V10 comparisons are by archetype. A missing line is not evidence. d27 is a rest day.

## 1. Verdict

Against V10 the group is quieter and more readable: the 51-Friends-to-Enemy flip and the four-Witness-lines-per-blow flood are gone, the back-room doc has patients, and the gun and buyer now act on a contract instead of brawling strangers. The stories that start are good (the unpaid forger, the buyer who raises a failed hit, the ex-con who skips her first shift and joins a gang).
They are still not picked up. Every trade that needs a walk to a spot (pimp, cult-pimp, gun on a contract, dodger to a clinic or seller) is cut mid-walk after 100 to 400 tiles and never arrives; the buyer's Hunt flips on and off every two hours without a step.
Jail is worse than V10 in one way a player would see: prisoners sit at energy 0.00 and starve, with nothing in the diary.

## 4. Bugs, ranked by what the player sees first

## 5. Dull spots, ranked

1. **The pimp and cult-pimp** (no Collect, no Preach). Mechanism: the Spot is chosen inside a bounded radius of the home or the gang's block, so the walk is under an hour; arrival starts the Collect or Preach, and a missed one is a note the boss reads.
2. **The gun with a contract and an empty week.** `gun_38550` never reaches RaidTarget; `gun_40332` runs a noodle bar. Mechanism: a taken contract plans a StakeOut at the target's door for a bounded time, with a deadline; an expired one posts `ContractLost` and a debt.
3. **The fixer who serves nobody** (`fixer_22056`). Mechanism: the forger and fixer shop bookings the camera shop already makes become Full walk-ins at shift start for a followed fixer, so ClerkWork has a customer line.
4. **Jail with no inside.** Seven days of `MetInJail` and nothing else. Mechanism: a cell offers a rationed meal, a cellmate's job or debt, or the crew's visit (the forger's referral line is the model); sleep and food are real inside, so Starving is a sentence's consequence the guard answers.
5. **The jobless doc and forger after an unpaid quit.** Both sit in a room for days. Mechanism: the quit stands as a grievance; the owner is visited (the forger tries, `d24 15:00`, and never arrives) to demand the owed 15c, becoming a Confronted line as in V10's `black_doc_13839`.
6. **The arms dealer and a missing arms trade.** Mechanism: the dealer's Muster hands arms to the crew for a raid; the sale is a line with a buyer.

- **Walking.** Travel is 10-15 h a day for `pimp_47630` (all 7 days) and `dodger_29048` (d24-d27), 0c earned. Reads as a man who commutes to nothing.
