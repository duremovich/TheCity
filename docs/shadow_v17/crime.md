## 1. Verdict

Better than V16: the new pickers find real doers (a bouncer in a gang crew, two gang leaders as arms dealers, a prisoner who killed), the target mechanisms fire in both `marked` diaries, and the cell has a day (meal, yard, kin, a fight that adds 3 days).
Still thin where the trade should be: the arms dealers sell nothing (`sold_dealer` 8 -> 0), one buyer is a silent exec, one forger earns 1c in a week, and the old walk the city for 9-23 h a day.
A leader's life reads as a ledger of Succession, war-call and lie-low lines rather than a story, and a hospital patient starves for two days under visitors.

## 5. Dull spots, ranked, with the mechanism

1. **Arms dealers sell nothing.** Mechanism: a dealer with stock sells to a named gang's or fixer's Job order, so the picker finds trades, not leaders; the gunsmith's surplus ends in a buy line the dealer's diary shows.
2. **The silent exec** (`buyer_103827`): 1,181c, 8.8 h of meetings a day. Mechanism: an exec with a rival posts a tag or a guard on a grudge; she has a `Standing` trauma contract on herself (`#509 trauma on Sable Bellamy ... posted d11`) that her diary never mentions.
3. **Jail beyond day one.** The meal and the yard are a rhythm now, but `jailed_86840` walks alone six days of seven among 3,042 prisoners. Mechanism: a cellmate who is a witness, debtor or enemy named by the prisoner's grudge list (the four enemies of `jailed_12739 d21 00:01` are never in the yard).
4. **The wounded target's three days** (bug 2): a kin who brings food, or a clinic bill that forces a decision.
5. **`dodger_14433`'s week** is witness lines and three thefts; the pursuit that gave `dodger_97292` a story (`d27 22:31 gave up the chase`) never reaches her. Mechanism: a witness who names the dodger to a guard.
6. **`gun_95034`** (70 y, no weapon): the hit goes to a man who cannot do it. Mechanism: a taker refuses a contract above his fighting skill, with the line, so the board stays honest.
