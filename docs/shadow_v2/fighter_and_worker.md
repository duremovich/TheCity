# Critique: fighter_2059 and worker_1686 (seed 42, d19-d25 diary, Life L2 build a737870)

Headline: the Fighter has a place to be and a nightly event, but the bout is a dice roll that happens without her, pays nothing, hurts nobody, and turns into a street "beating" that costs her a best friend. The Worker keeps office hours and a commute of 35 minutes, which is a large improvement over V1, but his wage does not feed him: he is starving by day 21 while employed by the richest corp in the city.

Diaries read whole (filtered of needs snapshots for the second pass). Sources in `scratchpad\shadow_v2\pinned\`. The week starts on a Friday (d19). Shift key 6 is the weekly rest day (`exec::routine::is_workday`), so d20 is the day off for both.

## Findings

### Fighter

1. **A pit bout is recorded as a street beating, so it follows the fighter like a cloud.** What: every bout loser gets a `Lost` memory against the winner. The game reads a `Lost` memory as the deed Assaulted, so the bout becomes a crime-shaped rumour. Evidence: `d19 23:00` "Bout: Lena Kimura beat Tristan Kowalczyk at Fight Pit#484 (bets paid 0)"; `d20 15:19` "GOSSIP ABOUT ME (as the doer): Tristan Kowalczyk told Rosalind Nakamura#825: Assaulted by Lena Kimura on Tristan Kowalczyk"; `d22 00:00` "Story: Nutrix Now: Lena Kimura assaulted Tristan Kowalczyk" and "Story: Civic Wire: ..."; `d21 00:00` "GrudgeFormed: Agathe Park swore against Lena Kimura (a beating, 0.50)" three times and "Agathe Park#965 is now Enemy (was Friend), affinity -1.00". Reputation moves from `dread 0.22 heat 0.04` (d19) to `dread 0.82 heat 0.23` (d22) with no crime. The Lena-as-victim version appears too ("Assaulted by Tristan Kowalczyk on Lena Kimura" at `d20 03:01`) because Tristan wins on d22 and d24. Cause: `systems/leisure.rs::bouts` calls `world.remember(loser, MemoryKind::Lost, Some(winner), ...)`; `systems/memory.rs::deed_of` maps `MemoryKind::Lost` to `Deed::Assaulted`; the gossip pool, the W10 kin channel (`gossip::kin`, the loser's spouse and friends are told), the news Story, `grudges.rs` (`Deed::Assaulted` becomes `GrudgeCause::Assaulted`, "a beating") and the reputation dread weight all consume it. Severity: **distorts the day** (a best friend lost to a game rule, plus grudges and heat from a sanctioned sport). Fix: give the bout its own memory kind or a "sanctioned" flag that `deed_of` skips; the kin channel, grudges and heat never see it. Let the bout feed a separate "Pit fame" value (dread as fame is fine, as a fighter wants it). Have the news report "Pit results" instead of "assaulted".

2. **Is 144 gossip tellings in a week plausible? The number is real but it is two separate things, and one is a bug.** What: the 144 in STATS is 31 (Lena) + 113 (Maren Upton, `fighter_1427`) + 0 (`fighter_1884`). The 31 are all the bout deed above: 29 tellings as doer, 2 as object, most between third parties that repeat each other ("(already known / dropped)" at `d23 20:09` and `d24 02:13`). The 113 are one real street beating (`d19 13:10` "Assault: Maren Upton attacked Tristan Kowalczyk") by Lena's spouse, who hunted Tristan from `d19 05:37` after a grudge from before the week. A beating of one man by one woman told 113 times is loud but it is a real crime, so that is tuning. 31 tellings of a nightly sporting event is not. Cause: finding 1, plus no cap on re-tellings of a deed between people who already hold it. Severity: the 31 **distort**; the 113 are cosmetic. Fix: finding 1, and stop a pair of holders re-telling a deed they already know.

3. **The bout happens without her, and on her day off.** What: Lena wins a bout at 23:00 while she is walking to a street corner in another district. Evidence: `d20 22:58` "ACTION Wander @ street (Mid East), 30 min", then `d20 23:00` "Bout: Lena Kimura beat Tristan Kowalczyk at Fight Pit#484" and "memory Won of Tristan Kowalczyk". d20 is the rest day (shift key 6) and she did no shift. On d23 and d24 she left the pit at `23:19`, but the bouts at `d23 23:00` and `d24 23:00` happened while she was there. Cause: `leisure::bouts` filters fighters on `Job::on_shift(tod)` and `living` only. It does not check `is_workday`, the fighter's position or the fighter's current plan. Severity: **blocks the role** (the bout is the point of a Fighter and she does not take part in it). Fix: the bout needs both fighters inside the pit building; on a rest day or an absent fighter it is cancelled or forfeited, with a memory and a missed bonus.

4. **Her job is ClerkWork, not fighting.** What: a six-hour "ClerkWork" action at the pit every evening, with no customers and no training. Evidence: `d19 18:02` "PLAN Work [ClerkWork]", "ACTION ClerkWork @ Fight Pit#484, 358 min (ended)" on d19, d21, d22, d25; 316 and 317 min on d23 and d24. The fighter's own `bout` is a single line in the event log. Cause: `goap/actions.rs:272` maps `Role::Host | Attendant | Cook | Fighter | Croupier | Concierge` to ClerkWork. This is the V1 ripperdoc finding again. Severity: **blocks the role**. Fix: a Fight action for the Fighter role (warm up, bout, cool down, 22:30-23:30) and a training action for off-bout hours, so the evening has a shape.

5. **A bout hurts nobody and pays nothing.** What: the loser loses 0.3 energy and 0.3 safety, and the winner gains 0.01 fighting. No injury, no scar, no wound, no purse, no winner's share. "bets paid 0" on all 7 nights. Evidence: Lena loses on d22: `d23 00:00` "energy 0.45 safety 0.76", next 02:00 "energy 0.55 safety 0.88". She loses on d24: `d25 00:00` "energy 0.00 safety 0.77", and by `d25 22:00` she is back at "energy 0.49 safety 1.00". Her wage comes "from the City" (`d20 00:00` "Wage +6 from the City"), not from the pit. Cause: `bouts()` applies a flat 0.3 and sets the bets from `v.bets`; nobody bets because nobody is at the pit before 23:00 except staff. Only the top two fighters on shift ever pair, so the same pair meets nightly; `fighter_1884` is at Fight Pit#473 where no pair exists, and has no bout all week. Severity: **distorts the day** (a fighter who never gets hurt or paid for it). Fix: a fight injury (a Wounded state with a Clinic cost and a recovery time, scaled by the loser's margin), a winner's purse out of the door and the bets, a rotating card so more fighters fight, and spectators (the free show exists in `leisure.rs:573` but nobody is in the room).

6. **A wage lost for leaving 41 minutes early.** What: on d23 and d24 she is paid nothing at midnight. Evidence: `d23 23:19` "plan interrupted: Work -> Sleep ... Sleep score 0.505, runners-up: Work 0.455" and the same on `d24 23:19` ("score 1.361"); there is no `Wage` line at `d24 00:00` or `d25 00:00` (payments on d20, d22 and d23 only: 3 of 6 workdays). Cause: `exec::actions::end_shift` marks the shift worked and adds `days_unpaid` only when ClerkWork finishes; a preempted action pays nothing. The shift runs to 24:00, which is Night, and Sleep's phase term rises at night. Severity: **distorts the day** (a lost 7c is a meal and a half). Fix: pay by the shift clock as `law::credit_guard_shifts` does for guards, or credit the shift once more than ~80% is worked; do not let Sleep outbid an in-shift Work unless energy is critical.

7. **A false start to work eats 1.5 hours.** What: she sets off for work two hours early, turns round, and sets off again. ## What already reads right (versus V1 and L1b)

- **Commute and sleep are real.** Emeric's commute is 33 to 37 minutes (V1: 4 to 6 h); Lena's 26 to 51. The longest sleeps are 7 h 13 min for Lena (`d25 00:10` 433 min) and 7 h 13 min for Emeric (`d19 00:01` 433 min). Neither is at energy 0 for more than an hour or two (V1 Worker: 36 h).
- **Wages at the workplace.** Emeric is paid at 18:00 in the Parlour (`d19 18:00` "+5c (wage)"), not at the Hall.
- **The week has a rhythm.** Both agents work the same hours six days and rest on d20 (V1: three days worked).
- **There is a fallback for the poor.** Scavenge exists and runs (V1 Homeless never begged). It pays 1c an hour, which is the right order for a last resort.
- **The street is alive at night.** `d20 02:00` "HANGOUT at street (Mid East) for 61 min: 6 other(s) there", `d20 23:30` 13 others, with named acquaintances and rumours passed on. Ties form (Rhys, Flux, Kasimir), and a stranger becomes a friend through repeated meetings (Sana Ferreira +0.13 to +0.28 for Emeric).
- **A grudge turns into a hunt.** Maren's feud with Tristan runs the whole chain (grudge, `HuntStarted`, AskAround, StakeOut, Attack, Assault, news). That is good emergence, and it is the reason finding 2's 113 tellings exist.
- **The Fighter's rival.** A nightly opponent who becomes a friend through shared work (Tristan +0.35 to +0.88 across the week) is a good detail. It is only good once the bout stops being an assault.
- **Hunger, and the money.** Hunger and Starving show up as events and memories. Food, rent and wage flows are all labelled, and the prices are readable.

## Verdicts

- **Lena Kimura, fighter: does not read as a fighter.** She is a clerk who is told the result of a fight she may not attend; it hurts nobody, pays nothing, and her best friend turns on her for it. Fix findings 1, 3, 4 and 5 and she would.
- **Emeric Ferreira, worker: reads as a worker who is being broken.** The day is believable (up early, 9 hours at a counter, one meal, a street corner), but the wage does not buy the week, hunger costs him his pay, and a rest day is a day of rubbish. Fix findings 12, 13 and 14 and he becomes a believable poor-but-employed man.
