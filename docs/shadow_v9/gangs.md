Cites are `<arch>_<id> dDD HH:MM` under `target/shadow_v9/isle100k_s42_d21/`. ## 1. Verdict

- **The gang layer is a list of day jobs with a gang badge.** Eleven of 14 hold a Vat Tech, Clerk, Researcher, Super or Club Staff post and live a worker's life; the gang shows up as a 4c tribute at 00:05, a rumour about someone else's extortion, or a 0-coin shakedown.
- **Where the gang does act, the walk eats it.** A member spends 10-16 h a day travelling inside one district (`gang_member_18708` 82 h in 7 days, 16 of 30 GoTo legs over 2 h, 7 over 4 h), and the one thing it plans all week, a Preach, never happens.
- **The stories are there but nothing in the sim picks them up.** Quit-a-creed, grudge, hunt, assault (`cultist_23160`) and join-a-gang, shakedown, brawl, arrest, ten days (`runner_22236`) are good lives; the victim just goes back to sleep, and the faith, the raid and the deal leave no trace.

## 4. Dull spots (a mechanism, not a knob)

1. **The faith is invisible.** 9,394 `Preached` events in 30 days (analyze100k) and no diary shows a preacher or a sermon; `Preached` lands at 00:05 as a system line seen by bystanders (`purist_104777 d23 00:08`, `scav_64630 d22 00:08`). Mechanism: a cultist's Preach is a scheduled service at a Chapel (a day and hour per creed), the congregation walks in and the diary of every attendee gets "heard Rin preach the Open Circuit", a tithe moves coins, and an attendee can convert or walk out. The creed-leaver in `cultist_23160` becomes a heretic the congregation hunts, which the sim already half does (GrudgeFormed, HuntStarted).
2. **Gang day jobs turn the gang into a hobby.** Eleven of 14 hold a wage post, including all three leaders. Mechanism: the gang's order takes shifts first: a Raid or Protect order puts a night duty on the member's schedule, with a pay (4c tribute is the whole of it today), and missing it costs standing and the post. A member then chooses between the boss and the boss at the Vat Farm, and the misery phase can tip the choice.
3. **Raids end in a fight and then nothing.** Mechanism: a Brawl writes a result (winner, wounded, a stolen stock or a smashed till), a grudge between the two crews, and a rumour at the target venue; the wounded go to the black doc or a clinic (phase 2). `purist_9861` already walks the whole raid; only the last page is blank.
4. **Hideout with no hospitality.** A gang of 2 with a Hideout holds Court for no one (`gang_leader_41566`). Mechanism: Court is called when 2+ members can attend, summons them by name at the previous night's end, and fails with a reason ("nobody came") otherwise; a small gang that cannot muster takes a rival's offer or folds, which gives GangLeave and Absorb something to do.
5. **Release and return.** `dealer_55991 d26 07:40` is out after 5 days and just goes home to HangOut seven times. Mechanism: release sends the member to his gang (debts, a new rank, a welcome-back or a "you talked" beating); the gang's treasury paying bail is a lever the sim already has coins for.
6. **A job lost to the cell.** `runner_22236` takes 10 days for Assault while holding a Researcher post (`Lab#5391`); `gang_leader_101227` is jailed twice for Fare Evasion and misses night shifts at the Club (`d22`, `d24` jail 10 h). Mechanism: absences from jail feed the employer's absence count (phase 1.1) and the story moves to "the runner has no job on release".
7. **Fare evasion is the leader's whole crime.** `gang_leader_101227` d21 11:36: one evasion with 26 Witness lines in the same minute (a crowd at a station), a report, an arrest at 21:38, a one-day sentence. It reads as noise, not a criminal. Mechanism: a leader's own crime is chosen for the gang's need (a stock run, a shakedown) and a fare is the cover, not the offence.
8. **Rest days are a HangOut at home alone.** `scav_64630 d27` 20 of 20 hangouts "nobody there"; `dealer_55991` 11 of 14. Mechanism: a rest day sends a gang member to the Hideout or the bar to be seen (order-driven), and a tag of the gang's rivals' turf to cross.
9. **Scavs have no scav's day.** Mechanism: the scav order picks a district's bins, a Harvest of a dead body or a lost-item find on the street, with a weekly value, so a scav diary reads as a trade (this is OPEN-176 and phase 2).
10. **Runners barely run.** `runner_81421`: 2 SellData in 7 days, +88c once, hack cooldown 2-10 days. Mechanism: a runner holds a contract chain from the gang or a fixer (steal this record), with a deadline and an ICE risk, so the 14.3 h travel day (`d27`) becomes errands.
