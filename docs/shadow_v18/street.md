## 1. Verdict

Better than V17, and the one big V17 bug is gone: a starving man now walks to a bin, a pot or a bowl, is told it ran dry and tries the next, and eats (every hunger <= 0.10 sample in the group ends in a `[meal]`). Lives are busier and the walking pace reads human; the poor are still harsh and thin, but a few of them now go somewhere (Guard, Attendant, Jailer, a funeral, a grudge).
The new noise is the Street Market theft carousel (a Guard off shift sees 25 thefts in two days) and a pile-on of corp "spin" contracts on one 18-year-old vendor.
No under-18 in any Assault/Fare/Arrest/Bout/Cells/Sentence/Death line (age scan of every line, 0 hits). But a 2-year-old lives on the street five days (bug 1) and a 10- and a 4-year-old go "2 days unfed" before the service acts (bug 2).
