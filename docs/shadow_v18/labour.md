## 1. Verdict
- Against V17: the pace made commutes human (home to post 1-7 min, 43 min for a 739-tile walk) and every employed diary is paid daily at shift end; the labour group reads **a little more like people** where a life has a gang or a crime in it (`night_shift_11299`, `worker_friday_34874`), and **the same or worse** where it is a shift (14 of 22 have no event beyond Witness/Hire/Cells lines).
- New and worst: the dawn gate now holds 2,300-2,470 people per yard (V17: 570-630), cabs are dead (11 of 12 shifts, no fare), and Cells names the wrong reporter (a grudge against an innocent).
- The Y1 hunger chain works once (soup line, `foreman_21729 d21`) but Eat loses to Work for 8 h before it fires; no one starved.
