# Shadow V3: the gray market and faith (forger, arms dealer, cultist; black doc and pimp)

Seed 42, days 19-26 and 90-97, pinned. Read as lives.

## Forger

**Zoe Osgood**, 33, Mid East, the Fixer's Forger. Sells papers for 192 coins in a week (the gray market's best earner in the pass), buys an Eyes implant, is reported for Forgery, has a guard on her payroll, is arrested anyway, her own papers exposed at the arrest, two days in the Jail, out again. 41 idle hours.

*Verdict:* a gray-market life with its risk: alive. **Gap:** her wage line runs 80 coins out against 15 in, which the diary does not explain (a flow to check in the shadowing session).

## Arms dealer

**Adela Ambrose**, 21 (day 19): the week in the Jail, all 168 hours (the only candidate was jailed). **Marek Haas**, 47 (day 90): an Office Worker at a Spire HQ by day and The Hollow's collector by night; one arms sale (10 coins); Habitat posts a locate and a 284-coin tag on him; contracts pass to takers; 12 grudges are sworn against him in the week.

*Verdict:* Marek is the most layered life of the pass (a corp clerk who runs a gang's money and is hunted by a corp). **Gap:** arms dealing is a sliver of it: one sale a week.

## Cultist

**Gwendolyn Fletcher**, 32, The Drowned Choir: abducts twice, deals stims, 120 hours in the Jail for Dealing, comes out and is hired as a Builder. **Greta Volkov**, 46, a Bartender in the Choir: extorts three Blocks for the cult, then walks away; the leader swears against her (a creed left, 0.8: the hunt threshold). **Lena Lindqvist**, 47 (day 90), a Super who tithes 15 of 25 coins of wage. **Silvio Upton**, 19 (day 90), a Fighter in the cult, a killing laid to him, 144 hours in the Jail, eight grudges.

*Verdict:* the cult reads as a gang with a creed: tithes, extortion, killing, apostates marked for the hunt. **Gaps:** no worship in the diaries (no Preach attended by a member in the week; the faith is a tithe line); The Drowned Choir is the city's cult and a scav gang at once, so the taboo on chrome and the harvest of chrome collide.
