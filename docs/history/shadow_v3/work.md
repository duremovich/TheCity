# Shadow V3: work (builder, foreman, loader, courier, cabbie, office worker, teacher)

Seed 42, days 19-26, pinned, two diaries per archetype (one foreman). Read as lives.

## Builder

**Bertram Kaneda**, 72, and **Ulysses Nightingale**, 65, both Builders at the Vats Construction Yard. Six shifts each, 6-8 coins a day, walking the Super's round of a Block on the side, 14-17 coins of fares a week. Ulysses's kin is killed in the week; he swears, goes for intel and stakes out the killer's door: a 65-year-old builder hunting a murderer after work. Net -1 to +6 coins for the week.

*Verdict:* working lives, poor. **Gap:** the city's builders are old: the seeded ages run to 75 and nothing retires a 72-year-old from a construction site (frailty slows the output, it does not end the job).

## Foreman

**Dorian Nakamura**, 19, Builder and the Yard's foreman (its longest-serving hand). Picks the crew at the Yard (the hire events name him), works his shifts, plays street dice, net +5.

*Verdict:* the role exists on paper. **Gap:** "longest-serving" picks a 19-year-old over the 72-year-old: tenure is days in the post, and the seeded city starts everyone at day 0.

## Loader

**Nerys Brandt**, 48, night Loader at the Freight Yard. In the week she joins The Drowned Choir; she abducts Quinn Ellery in the Vats, rips his implant, he dies, she strips his body for 67 coins and the dead man's kin swears against her. She buys an Arms implant with the money; the cult expels her for chrome. All while keeping her night shifts.

**Corwin Underhill**, 23, night Loader, joins the same cult and steals three motorcycles in a week, fences them, is reported, and posts a guard on himself through the Fixer.

*Verdict:* two of the most alive diaries of the pass, and both because of the gangs, not the job. The Yard shift is five ClerkWork blocks a week. **Gap:** a loader moves nothing visible (the handling fee is a flow; no crate is lifted in a diary).

## Courier

**Fenn Hallow**, 45, and **Lorcan Sallow**, 41, Couriers at the Vats Freight Yard. 18 deliveries each (GoTo the target's home), 60-72 hours of the week walking, 6-8 coins a day. Lorcan was robbed on day 14 and beaten on day 6; the robbery is laid to a named thief, the beating never will be.

*Verdict:* a courier's week is walking, and this one is: real. **Gap:** a parcel is not carried (nothing in the inventory), and the routes ignore the courier's home (Lorcan lives in Sump East, the Yard is in the Vats).

## Cabbie

**Brenna Reyes**, 38, and **Cutter Haas**, 38, Cab Drivers. They work ClerkWork shifts at the Garage; neither drives anyone in the week. Cutter, on the night shift, quits unpaid.

*Verdict:* **not alive**: the cab trips are Statistical (55 a seed over 120 days); a body on a cab shift stands at the counter. **Gap:** the Full cab driver needs a fare to drive (a rider's hail, a route, the fare at the door).

## Office worker

**Tomas Reyes**, 64, and **Kestrel Abernathy**, 67, Office Workers at Spire Corp HQs. Six shifts, 46-47 coins a week, home in the Spire, 30-39 Rests and three venue visits each. Kestrel drinks at a Bar three times.

*Verdict:* a quiet salaried life; the Spire's old clerks. **Gap:** 40-50 idle hours a week (Rest) outside the shift: nothing to do after work in the Spire but rest.

## Teacher

**Odile Blackwood**, 53, and **Kieran Fairweather**, 48, Teachers at the two Schools. Full shifts, 35 coins a week, evenings at venues and hang-outs.

*Verdict:* a working life, but a teacher with no class: no child is named in either diary. **Gap:** the School's children are counted off screen (`ages::daily`), and Schools sit far from where the children live (see `docs/GOD_SCENARIOS_V12.md` gap 11).
