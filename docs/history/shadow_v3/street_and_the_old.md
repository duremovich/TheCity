# Shadow V3: the street and the old (vendor, street worker, elder, scav)

Seed 42, days 19-26 (vendor, elder, scav) and 90-97 (street worker, scav), pinned. Diaries from `citysim-cli shadow --seed 42 --start-day 19 --days 7 --count 2 --pick ...` (the commands are in `docs/SHADOW_V3.md`). Read as lives, not as systems.

## Vendor

**Fenn Penhallow**, 36, Mid West, no post. Sleeps at home, scavenges at dawn for nothing, then at 09:00 the Pitch goal wins (0.93) and he walks **153 minutes** to his spot and sells for an hour at a time: 49 Vend hours in the week, 26 coins of meals sold, 18 coins of wholesale, net **+5 coins** for the week. Rent short twice. 43 hours of the week are walking. He has a spouse at affinity 0.64 he never sees.

**Verity Moreau**, 63, Sump Central. 55 Vend hours, 49 meals sold for 94 coins against 51 of wholesale: a real business with a thin margin, net +13. Her spot is near her home, so she walks less (34 h) and hangs out between sales at her own pitch's corner (30 HangOuts).

*Verdict:* Verity reads as a street seller. Fenn reads as a man commuting two and a half hours to sell one bowl an hour. **Gaps:** the pitch spot ignores the walk (the pitch is placed where the district's cap allows, not near home); the margin is thin enough that a bad week is a missed rent.

## Street worker

**Philippa Duarte**, 22, Sump West (day 90). Her Street Worker pitch closes when the foreman of a Fight Pit hires her as a Fighter, and she moves nearer work. The rest of the week is a fighter's: shifts, two assaults on her, a grudge, a Hunt she starts twice (a man she wants found), a tithe to the cult. 25 meals in her pocket.

*Verdict:* the street work is the rung she climbs off within the week, which is what the spec wanted. **Gap:** no street worker stays on the street long enough to be shadowed: 0 candidates on day 19, one on day 90. The pimp's cut, the sweeps and the dangers of the trade never reach a diary.

## Elder

**Sabine Mbeki**, 65, a Fab Tech on the night shift in the Vats. Works five of seven nights, strips two bodies she finds (9 and 14 coins), is reported for Theft and jailed three days, and buys a motorcycle on finance with the loot. A hard old woman who works nights and takes what lies on the street.

**Amara Voss**, 65, Mid West, no post, 0 coins. 27 Scavenge actions, three dumpster dives, 12 coins found, rent short. 37 idle hours. Nobody takes her in (no child in the city).

*Verdict:* both read as old lives in this city: one still working, one scavenging. **Gaps:** no pension, by design; but no friend, neighbour or church takes a childless elder in, and the Mission cot is not where she goes.

## Scav

**Bram Reyes**, 47, Sump Central, no post, 3 coins. A member of The Drowned Choir (a scav gang and a cult at once). He abducts Sable Brandt in Sump West, rips one implant from her at the Hideout and she dies on the table. A witness reports him; he leans on the witness and the report is withdrawn. Then he walks away from the Choir and its leader swears against him (a creed left). He strips a corpse for nothing, steals, attacks. 42 HangOuts and 19 Scavenges fill the rest: a killer who is still poor.

**Leopold Sato**, 74, Sump Central: marries in the week, abducts and kills Sebastian Fairweather, is reported, arrested and jailed for 106 hours. **15 grudges** are sworn against him: the dead man's kin and friends. The city remembers.

Day 90: **Rue Bellamy**, 24, extorts a Block for 5 coins and is detained by a Security robot (6 days for Shakedown); **Hollis Carrow**, 38, a Vat Tech in the same gang, works his shifts and sells stims; the gang's scav business never touches him.

*Verdict:* the scav reads as Dylan asked: a gang that kills people for chrome and organs, poor members doing the killing, and a city that answers with grudges and arrests. **Gaps:** the killer earns nothing (the take goes to the gang's stock; the organs rot); a 74-year-old abducts and wins the fight (fight odds read the crew, not age); the cult that forbids chrome is the gang that rips it (The Drowned Choir expels a member for the implant it paid her to install).
