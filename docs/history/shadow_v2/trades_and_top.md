# Critique: ripperdoc_1337, fabber_792, reporter_1631, runner_1287, ceo_757 (seed 42, d19-d25 diary, main at a737870)

Headline: the Fab tech is the first trade in the set that reads as a job and an evening. The ripperdoc, the reporter and the runner still do not show their trades, and the CEO is a salaried office worker with a better postcode. Three of the five days are broken by the same two causes (a commute flap and a Market that is hours away), and the reporter is blocked outright by two bugs.

## Mags Mercer, ripperdoc (Corp, 50, wage 8/day at Ripperdoc#443 in the Spire, Zetatech staff, spouse Seraphine Kimura)
A day (d21): 00:00 Sleep fragments, 02:11 walks 83 min to a street spot, HangOut with one acquaintance, 04:37 walks 81 min home to eat. 07:35 sets out for the Clinic, turns back at 07:47, walks home, Rests, sets out again 08:05, arrives 08:24. 09:02 ClerkWork as one 538 min action. 18:00 +7 c wage (net +6 after rent). 18:03 walks 115 min to a Civic Gambling Den, a 2 c drink, a 2 c win, 99 min home, sleeps from 22:56. Net +6 c, travel 7.0 h. d20 is the rest day: a 203 min walk to the Civic Market, 8 h of 30-min Wanders, a 201 min walk home. d22 and d24 are 9 h shifts then 4.5 h of 90-min Rests at home. d25 walks out mid-shift for food and logs 12.4 h of travel. Week: 5 paid shifts, 44.3 h travel, 31.4 h idle, net -23 c, no patient ever named.

### Findings

1. **No patient, and the clinic serves without her.** What: not one Treat, Install or client line in her week; the closing summary says "counts: none". Yet Ripperdoc#443 did sell care: ceo_757 took Therapy there at d20 09:43 and 10:46 (60 c each) while Mags was wandering Civic on her rest day, and again at d21 17:49 (54 c) while Mags sat in ClerkWork (d21 09:02-18:00) and logged nothing. All three fees went to corp Zetatech ("Treatment -60 to corp Zetatech"); she got her 7 c. Why: the profession is a wage and a chair. The customer side exists, the provider side does not. Cause: `assets::seller_open` is true when any staff member's shift clock is on, not when the doctor is present, so the Clinic is "open" with an empty counter; `ClerkWork` is the only job action (`goap/actions.rs:266`) and takes no customer. Severity: blocks the role. Fix: open only when a staff member is at the counter, make the treatment a two-party action (the doctor's ClerkWork turns into Treat-the-customer for its duration), write a Client memory and edge on both sides, and pay the doctor a cut of the fee on top of the wage floor.
2. **Nine hours as one action.** What: ClerkWork runs 537-538 min with a single need snapshot every 2 h and no meal, no break, no walk-in. Why: even with no customers, the day reads as a clock block. Cause: ClerkWork is one long action (`exec/actions.rs` end_shift). Severity: cosmetic until finding 1 lands, then it must become a wait-and-serve loop. Fix: chunk the shift into hour slices that can be pre-empted by a customer or a meal.
3. **The morning commute flaps.** What: d21 07:35 Work, 12 min walk, 07:47 "Work -> Idle", 11 min back home, Rest 2 min, 08:05 Work again. The same on d22 07:35 and d24 07:35. Why: she walks to the door and back every working morning, and sleeps in 1-10 min fragments around it (d22 04:08-05:08 five Sleeps of 1-9 min). Cause: `routine::must_leave_for_work` tests `until <= travel_estimate(from here) + margin`. As she steps outside, the estimate shrinks, the test flips to false, the plan drops and Idle takes her home, where the estimate is long again. Severity: distorts the day. Fix: latch the commute once it starts (keep the plan until arrival or the shift ends), or leave with a margin that does not shrink with distance.
4. **Food is 150-203 minutes away.** What: no Market exists in the Spire. Eat plans go to Street Market#413 (Civic): d20 02:50 203 min, d23 00:28 199 min (then Work interrupts and she never buys), d25 01:17 150 min and d25 15:47 187 min out of her own shift (work 6.7 h that day), and the way back is another 203 min (d25 19:17). Why: a Corp resident's day is mostly corridor. Cause: the three Markets in the whole diary set are Civic 413, Mid East 414 and Mid West 412; the Spire has none, and Eat scores nearest-Market only. Severity: distorts the day, the single biggest time sink (travel 44.3 h of 168). Fix: a grocer or Market in the Spire (and in Sump), EatOut at the Club or Lounge, a weekly stock-up, and an Eat plan that refuses a trip over about 40 min unless starving.
5. **A Corp wage that cannot feed her.** What: wage 7 c (6 after rent); 3 meals cost 12 c; Club entry 12 c is two days' pay; she ends d25 at 15 c and -23 c for the week. Why: a Zetatech employee on a 13,074 c treasury lives like a Dreg. Cause: `wage_ripperdoc` is flat; there is no fee income to the doctor (finding 1). Severity: distorts. Fix: revenue share; wages that scale with the employer's treasury.
6. **Days on end with no one.** What: spouse Seraphine Kimura (Spouse, +1.00) is never seen; intimacy sits at 0.88-1.00; belonging falls to 0.59. The only contacts are Odile Quarry (2 chats, d20) and two names at a HangOut. d22 and d24 evenings are 4.5 h of 90-min Rests at home. Cause: no household goal (still open from V1). Severity: distorts. Fix: a "see someone I love" goal that sends the spouse home or to a shared evening.

### Reads right versus V1 and L1b
The quit-unpaid and rehire cycle is gone (no Quit or Hire line all week). The off-shift wage chase is gone (no trip for owed pay). The wage is paid at the workplace at 18:00 on shift days. She has a Club evening (d19, d23) and a Gambling Den evening (d21). Idle is 31 h for the week. Energy never hits zero.

Verdict: still a clerk with a medical job title. Wages exist now; customers exist; but the two never meet, and the doctor is the one person in the building who is never part of the sale.

## Sebastian Coldwater, Fab tech (Corp, 32, wage 7/day at Parts Fab#441 in the Spire, Militech staff, sociability 0.99)

## Vivienne Quarry, reporter (Dreg, 39, knowledge 0.98, homeless, wage 4/day at Feed#452, Civic)
A day (d19): 00:01 walks 219 min to the squat in Sump West, sleeps. 07:43 sets out for Feed#452, a 403 min walk through Mid West and Mid East. 14:26 arrives at "Civic Hall#420" (not the Feed), Rests 1 min, "PLAN FAILED: Work failed at Rest: PreconditionLost", "Work unplannable: Unreachable". 14:41 Scavenges 61 min in Civic. 16:14 walks 401 min back to the squat. Travel 17.1 h. d20-d25: she never leaves Sump West again except on three aborted starts (d21 20:18, d22 06:41, d23 20:40, d24 01:15). The rest of her week is 60-min Scavenge blocks, one meal (4 c, d23 00:26), two Starving events (d20 19:41, d24 05:12), +6 c from scavenging, net +2 c.

### Findings

1. **She is hired from across the city.** What: a homeless Dreg from Sump West holds a Reporter job at the Civic Wire, a 400 min walk each way (d19 07:43-14:26). Why: no one chose her for her desk; they chose her for her number. Cause: `demography::pick_candidate` for a Feed sorts only by knowledge (0.98) over every free adult in the city, ignoring distance and home. Severity: blocks the role. Fix: filter to a commute cap (about 60 min) and prefer applicants with a home near the Feed.
2. **A city-owned Feed has no reachable workplace.** What: on d19 her GoTo(Workplace) lands at Civic Hall#420 (target Feed#452), the Rest there fails, and every later Work plan is unplannable: d21 09:11, 11:11, 13:11, 15:11, 17:11; d22 16:43; d23 09:11, 11:11, 13:11, 15:11; d24 10:51; d25 09:11, 11:11, 13:11, 15:11, 17:11. Why: she cannot ever begin a shift. Cause (likely, not run): Feed#452 is the Civic Wire, seeded with `owner None` (`news::seed_feeds`). For an unowned employer `World::wage_desk` returns the Hall, so `wage_at` becomes Hall, the extra `Workplace` distance entry in `goap/actions.rs:661-666` is skipped, and `is_l2_building` covers leisure venues and Fabs but not Feeds. Check that Lab#430 and any other unowned employer do not share the fault. Severity: blocks the role. Fix: always give `Workplace` the employer's own door for Feed, Lab and Fab; keep the Hall only as the pay desk.
3. **A job she never works, never loses and is never paid.** What: no wage, no dole line in seven days, although the plain homeless pick (homeless_1829) collects +4 c at 09:00 each day. She is "starving" twice and eats once. Why: she is neither employed nor poor on paper. Cause: the seeded Job has `paid_once: true` (`world.rs:1358`), so she is not dole-eligible by the first-wage rule; `days_unpaid` only grows in `end_shift`, so the two-day rule never fires; the quit rule needs unpaid days or memories. Nothing fires an absent worker. Severity: blocks the role and starves the person. Fix: dole eligibility for a Job holder who has not worked a shift in two days, and a no-show dismissal after a week so the post reopens.
4. **There is no beat even if she arrived.** What: the Reporter's shift is ClerkWork at the Feed; stories are written by `news::daily` from the day's pools, and only the staff's mean knowledge (not their presence or whereabouts) shapes the result (`systems/news.rs:692-700`). Reach counts "reporters who worked yesterday" (zero here). Why: a reporter who works would sit at a desk and report nothing. Cause: no field action for the role. Severity: missing feature. Fix: a Cover action: pick the day's top pool entry, walk to its district, witness, file; then sources, a byline and standing as reward.
5. **Scavenging is a 1 c every 3 hours.** What: roughly 40 Scavenge blocks yield 6 c. Cause: `scavenge_p` 0.15 is the L1b lever for exactly this; the cost lands on the one person with no dole. Severity: by design, but see finding 3. Fix: none beyond finding 3.

### Reads right versus V1 and L1b
V1 had no Reporter. L1b had no Feed. The Courted memories with Hollis Sato (+0.63 to +0.93) are the one good thing in the week.

Verdict: a reporter in name only. She is hired by the wrong rule, lives in the wrong place, cannot enter her own office, and is not paid or fed by the city while she holds the post. Fix findings 2 and 3 before judging the role.

## Roxy Lindqvist, runner (Street, 45, hacking 0.81, courage 0.18, Deck T1, jobless, 1 c)
Her whole life in the pin: 00:00 released from the Precinct after a DataTheft sentence. 00:28 to 06:16 five 61 min HangOuts at the same street spot, "nobody there". 07:19 Earn (Wander, Scavenge) fails twice with PreconditionLost. 08:23 Hack (score 0.149; Unwind 0.130). 08:24 sits down at a Bar#416 chair, 08:27 jacks in on Lab#430 Data, 08:51 fried and flatlined. 09:23 Hex Quarry strips the body (0 coins). d20 09:26 Bertram Ostrowski buries her. Nothing else in the diary.

### Findings

1. **A fatal first-hour gamble: partly by design, partly a missing fear.** What: the death is the sim's own dice (flatline needs a fry first, then the kill roll; at ICE 3 with a large gap it is 0.8, `config.rs` `p_flatline`), so a death is brutality by doctrine. But she was not a first-timer: the diary opens with "JAILED for DataTheft until d19 00:27", so a previous run had already ended in a trace or capture. Her fear is weak: the `1-p_fry x (1-courage)` line reads 0.81 -> 0.89 (a route fry chance near 0.2), and it feeds `virt.rs:1309` through a linear curve `0.6x + 0.4`, so it can lower the goal to 40 % but never to zero; there is no term for kill odds or for the deck-versus-ICE gap, and the goal beat Unwind by 0.02. A courage-0.18 woman with 1 c took an unrouted 20-30 % death risk on a hardened Lab with a tier-1 deck. Severity: distorts (brutal by design, but it reads as a dice roll, not as a person). Fix: a death-risk term (`p_fry x p_kill(ice, gap)`) that can veto for a cautious agent who is not desperate; a gap guard (never route a node whose `gap >= flatline_gap` unless wealth is critical); a visible memory of the earlier jail term in the Hack score.
2. **Why she was desperate: the streets had nothing.** What: both Scavenge attempts end at once with PreconditionLost (07:50, 08:22), the goal is cooled for 2 h, and Hack takes over. Cause (likely): Civic streets are swept, so there is no litter to scavenge (`litter` and `sweep_done`). A cleaner city leaves a jobless runner with one earner. Severity: distorts. Fix: a Scavenge fallback that uses the Bar or Market back-lot, and a fixer who hands out RunOrders (the V1 recommendation, still not built).
3. **Six hours alone at the same spot.** What: five HangOuts of 61 min at spot (151,63), "nobody there", while belonging climbs from 0.19 to 0.99 and four friends at +1.00 (Leopold Yardley, Rhys Stroud, Silvio Carrow, Matthias Santoro) never appear. Cause: the HangOut spot is a fixed coordinate, not chosen toward known contacts. Severity: distorts. Fix: pick the spot nearest a contact's usual place, fall back to a Bar.
4. **Nobody mourns her in the record.** What: she is stripped within 32 min by Hex Quarry (a stranger, 0 coins), buried the next morning by Bertram Ostrowski (not among her top ten edges), and the closing stats show 0 gossip about her. Why: four +1.00 friends and no reaction. Cause: (a) the pin tool ends the diary at death, so a mourner's reaction would be invisible even if it existed, and (b) I found no bereavement path for a Flatline in the code I read. Severity: missing feature, and a tool gap. Fix: Death memories and a mood hit for edges at Friend or above, a gossip seed ("flatlined in Lab#430's ICE") and a news line; the tool should keep logging events where the dead agent is the object for a few days.
5. **This is not the runner archetype V1 asked for.** What: no fixer, no RunOrder, no sell-and-upgrade loop; the "runner" is a jobless 1 c woman who runs on her own. Severity: missing feature. Fix: as in V1 (fixer-fed RunOrders, standing run goal, a deck loop).

### Reads right versus V1
V1's "runner" was a guard who never touched a deck. This one has a deck, hacks, and is killed by ICE with a readable log (JackedIn, Fried, Flatlined, Death, Stripped, Burial). The brutality is on tone.

Verdict: the permadeath is fine; the missing pieces are the fear that should make a cautious woman hesitate, and the world that should notice she is gone.
