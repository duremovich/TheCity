## Findings

1. **Neither role does the work, and nobody is served.** What: the cook's whole job is one `ClerkWork` action per day ("`d23 09:03` ACTION ClerkWork @ Noodle Bar#485 (Sump Central), 537 min"); the club hand's is "`d24 18:02` ACTION ClerkWork @ Club#469 (Vats), 358 min". No diary line shows a patron, an order, a tip, a bowl, a bouncer moment or a coworker. Across all 41 pinned diaries there is one `EatOut` and no one at all in Club#469 or Noodle Bar#485 besides the staff. Why: the role is the pay slip and a place to stand; a reader sees a clerk with a different building name. Cause: `ActionKind::work_for` maps `Role::Cook`, `Host`, `Attendant`, `Fighter`, `Croupier` and `Concierge` to `ClerkWork` (goap/actions.rs:272), the generic counter shift; `EatOut` needs `stock_food > 0` and a patron who chose that venue (systems/leisure.rs:1018), and nothing makes the Noodle Bar's stock or ties the staff to the patrons who do come (other diaries show 22 Enjoy actions at clubs, so patrons exist, just not here). Severity: blocks the role. Fix: a staff counter action that is driven by arrivals. The cook converts Food goods into the bar's `stock_food` during the shift and serves each `EatOut`; the club hand greets, checks and tips on each `Enjoy`. Each service writes a `Served` memory and a Client edge for the staff member and credits a tip. A staff meal comes from the same stock (see 5).

2. **The club hand's commute is 147 minutes each way.** What: "`d21 14:43` GoTo(Workplace) @ Block#180 (Mid East) -> Club#469 (Vats), 147 min (done)" and "`d24 00:02` GoTo(Home) @ Club#469 (Vats) -> Block#180 (Mid East), 147 min". Travel 8.1-11.9 h on every shift day, 60 h in the week against 35.5 h of work; sleep 4.2-5.7 h. The cook's 35-75 minutes each way is tolerable by comparison. Why: a 58-year-old whose day is five hours of walking and four hours of sleep is not a club worker, he is a walker who also holds a job. Cause: still the V1 cross-cutting cause 1. `demography::hire_candidate` and housing ignore distance, so a Mid East resident holds a Vats venue job. Severity: distorts the day (it would block the role for anyone who read the hours). Fix: a commute cap of about 60 minutes in hiring; if a vacancy has no local candidate, the venue stays understaffed or hires from the district.

3. **The departure predicate flaps, so he walks toward work and back.** What: "`d24 12:10` GOAL Work (was Unwind): score 0.867", "`d24 12:40` GOAL Unwind (was Work): score 0.210; runners-up: Idle 0.050" (Work has dropped out entirely), "`d24 13:10` GOAL Work (was Unwind): score 0.839", "`d24 15:40` GOAL Unwind (was Work)", "`d24 16:10` GOAL Work". He walks 30, back 30, forward 150, back 30, forward 81. The same happens on d19, d22 and d25. The cook shows the same shape at dawn: "`d21 06:18` Work", "`d21 06:48` Earn", "`d21 07:18` Work", with a hundred minutes spent on legs that cancel. Why: the commute reads as dithering, and it burns about two hours a day for the club hand. Cause: `must_leave_for_work` (exec/routine.rs:115) is a hard threshold, `until <= travel_estimate + 60`, and `travel_estimate` is Manhattan x 1.5 (exec/routine.rs:72). The estimate is about double the real walk (he leaves 350 minutes ahead for a 147-minute walk). It also shrinks 1.5x per tile covered, while the shift clock shrinks 1x, so after one 30-minute leg the predicate goes false and Unwind takes over. Turning back raises the estimate, and the predicate flips true again. Severity: distorts the day. Fix: once a commute leg starts, latch it until arrival (a `Work` plan owns the walk, as `raid_plan` owns its chain); fit the estimate to the real speed or use the real path length.

4. **Leaving the counter to eat or sleep forfeits the wage.** What: the cook is paid +4, +3, +5 and +4 on d19, d22, d23 and d25, and nothing on d21 and d24. On both days "`d21 16:18` plan interrupted: Work -> Eat" and "`d24 16:18` GOAL Eat (was Work): score 0.822; runners-up: Work 0.814" ended the shift 100 minutes early, on a 35- or 75-minute walk to eat. The club hand leaves 20 minutes early on d19: "`d19 23:40` GOAL Sleep (was Work): score 0.491; runners-up: Work 0.457", and the d20 00:00 pass shows only "Rent -1" with no wage. Why: two of the cook's six shifts and one of the club hand's six pay nothing, a third of the cook's week of pay, on a wage that barely feeds him. A shift that quits 100 minutes early for lunch is not a cook. Cause: a shift action is interruptible by any goal that outscores Work, and the Eat/Sleep scores have no knowledge that the wage is paid at shift end. Severity: distorts the day. Fix: a minimum commitment to a shift action, interrupt only for starving (hunger below 0.1) or danger, pay pro rata for hours worked, and let Eat and Sleep run after the shift end.
