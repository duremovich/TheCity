Slightly better than V15 in the people-in-a-harsh-city sense: one robbery now runs a full chain (woke short, told a stranger, the thief "learned who put him inside"), the builders are no longer sent 200 tiles a day, and a household bought food for a 3-year-old as a goal. The first thing a player sees is still the shift floor: loaders, fabbers, couriers and day labourers show a metronome with a Witness line a day.
