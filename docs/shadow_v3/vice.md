# Shadow V3: vice (joytoy, companion, madam, dancer)

Seed 42, days 19-26 and 90-97, pinned. Every visit, shift and tip is a timed record on a struct; the diaries show times, places and coins. Read as lives.

## Joytoy (a chip contract's Host at the Dollhouse)

**Marek Bellamy**, 30, and **Marek Ellery**, 33 (day 19); **Cato Kimura**, 57, and **Lena Voss**, 36 (day 90). Evening shifts at the Vats Dollhouse, 39-50 coins a week (the chip contract's premium), homes far away (Mid East, the Spire: 35-54 hours of travel a week). Marek Ellery strips a body for 12 coins and buys a deck; Lena's motorcycle is stolen twice in a week and she buys another on finance. Cato moves nearer work.

*Verdict:* the shift is a post with a premium, and the diaries read as ordinary night-shift lives. **Gaps:** the chip's cost to the person (the memory skip, the load) is invisible in the diary; the contract's debt, the reason they signed, never shows (no debt line, no creditor met).

## Companion

**Aldo Singh**, 39, the Spire Lounge: assaults a man and spends 104 hours of the week in the Jail; marries in the same week. **Volt Santoro**, 31, same Lounge: 56 coins of tips against 24 of wage in a week, a Legs implant on finance.

*Verdict:* Volt reads as the job: tips are most of the money. **Gap:** 45 Rests and 62 idle hours a week; the clients are coins, never names.

## Madam

**Rex Novak**, 52, Spire, Madam at the Civic Brothel: 63 coins a week, 60 idle hours, 34 Rests, four Enjoys of his own. *Verdict:* a well-paid manager with nothing to manage. **Gap:** the madam's hiring of street workers (`relief_hire`) never happens in the week (no street worker to hire).

## Dancer

**Zane Duarte**, 25, Civic Club: 51 idle hours, no events in the week. **Dagger Brandt**, 46, Vats Club: joins The Drowned Choir and tithes 12 of 29 coins of wage.

*Verdict:* the dancer row reads as before: a shift and empty days. **Gap:** the dancer's ladder (club owner, fixer, companion) never moves in a week; the cult is the only door that opens.
