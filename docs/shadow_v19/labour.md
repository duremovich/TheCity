Group: worker, worker_friday, night_shift, jobless, fabber, loader, builder, foreman, courier, cabbie, day_labourer (two each, 22 diaries). 100k island, seed 42, d21-27, main 41c31b2. Method: camera tables and Notable events of all 22 (scripted), CLIMB census across the group (407 lobby-wait lines, ~850 climbs), keyword greps (raid, landlord, rent, evict, floor, Fare, DawnPick, Wage), full days read: `night_shift_26883` d21, `builder_60752` d22, `builder_52838` d23 eve, `worker_53659` d22, `cabbie_71858` d21, `day_labourer_91938` d23, `fabber_48104` d25-26, `jobless_14715` d21-23. V18's report: `docs/shadow_v18/labour.md`.

## 1. Verdict
- Against V18 the labour lives read **more like people in a vertical city on the floor plan, and about the same in the shift**: a flat on floor 15 or 48, a lift (or 14 flights of stairs), a neighbour crowd on the landing, and a post that is a floor of a Bar are all new and mostly sane. The shift itself (Rest, then ClerkWork/Unload loops, one 534-minute FabWork block) is as dull as V18.
- Fixed since V18: the dawn gate no longer swallows thousands (56-62 sent home, V18 2,300-2,470), no one starves, no minor is harmed, no leg runs past 81 min.
- Still dead: cabbies (12 of 12 shifts "no fare, no pay", both diaries). New and worst: a 74 y man hired, chipped and fired every midnight (`jobless_14715`), and a 66 y cook who walks an hour to a hangout (`worker_40474`).

## 3. The vertical city at the player level
- **Reads like a place:** flats on floor 3, 10, 15, 21, 30, 48 with a floor range per building ("floors 0-59"), 273 + 77 + 37 + 20 lift climbs with a lobby wait, stairs for short climbs ("floor 0 to 1, 1 min"), a 14-floor stair descent in 4 min (`fabber_46993 d21 23:25`, a 14-floor block with no lift line), neighbours on a landing (HANGOUT on floor 15 with 13 others), shops on floor 0 and a Gambling Den / Parts Fab / Club / Construction Yard as tenants on floors 1-2 of a Bar (`worker_friday_31293`, `day_labourer_91938`).
- **The lift is a place only on paper.** Of 407 lobby-wait lines only 14 wait 5 min and 1 waits more than 1 min with a queue (`cabbie_86282 d23 16:16` floor 0 to 35, 5 min waiting, 7 in all). Everything else is "1 min waiting (1 called the car)" at every hour, including the 48th floor of the Spire (4 min flat, `night_shift_26883` every climb). A 15-person call at `loader_82044 d21 00:01` (floor 30, shift start) still waits 1 min. No rush-hour queue shows in this group.
- **Absurd or odd:**
  - A Construction Yard and a Parts Fab and a Gambling Den as floors of a Bar: `day_labourer_91938` climbs a Bar's stairs 0 to 1 and down again 20 times a day for the Unload loop (143 CLIMBs in 7 days). The stairs are sane; a yard on an upper floor is odd.
  - A Bouncer who does 353 min of ClerkWork (`worker_friday_31293 d21 18:05, 21:46`); a Cook with 59 Wander and 12 ClerkWork and no cooking (`worker_40474`). The job label and the action have little to do with each other.
  - Flat numbers vary (flat 1-7); no "every flat 1". No body above its building's top floor.
  - Landlord, floor-rent, "taken back after 7 days" and founding lines: **not seen** in these diaries; rent appears as "Rent -1 to corp Habitat N" once a week (`builder_60752 d25 00:00`, `courier_37806 d23 00:00`). A missing line is not evidence, but a labour life never meets its landlord.
