# Shadow V4: labour

Archetypes: worker, worker_friday, night_shift, fabber, loader, builder, foreman, courier, cabbie. 53 diaries: `k2_s43_d26_full` (2k, days 26-33), `k2_s44_d90_full` (2k mature, days 90-97), `k20_s42_d20` (20k, days 20-27). The 2k findings use the Full-LOD regenerations only. Read as lives.

Evidence is cited as `run/file dDD HH:MM`, with the runs shortened to **s43** (`k2_s43_d26_full`), **s44** (`k2_s44_d90_full`) and **k20** (`k20_s42_d20`).

- 15 of 50 labourers are over 60, and 8 of them are 71-74.

## Gaps, ranked by how much they distort a life

1. **Sleep is not tied to the night** `[bug]`
   - *Evidence:* day workers go to bed at 19:00-21:00, wake at 23:30-01:00 and walk 1.5 h to a corner in the small hours. One eats at 04:26 and goes back out; one steals a bike at 05:48. Sleep averages 5.1-6.5 h (`s43/builder_1410 d28 00:07-07:26`, `s44/fabber_1089 d92 01:39-06:39`).
   - *Cause:* `systems/leisure.rs::considerations` gates Unwind on `off_hours` with no Night-phase gate. Socialise in `utility/goals.rs` has one (`phase != Night`). Sleep also ends at full energy whatever the clock.
   - *Fix:* gate Unwind with `phase != DayPhase::Night` for day-shift holders, as Socialise does.
2. **A round that ends in someone else's building loses the afternoon shift** `[bug]`
   - *Evidence:* every courier (2-8 ClerkWork failures a week, at both city sizes), plus builders on the Super's round.
   - *Cause:* the location fallback in `goap/world_state.rs` uses `LocationKey::of_building`, which maps any Club, NoodleBar, SecurityOffice, Fab and the like to `Workplace`. The planner then plans ClerkWork where the agent stands; ClerkWork fails PreconditionLost twice and Work cools for 2 h.
   - *Fix:* return `Workplace` only when `b == job.employer`, otherwise `Street`.
3. **Low-urgency goals starve in the plan queue at 20k** `[bug]`
   - *Evidence:* ReportCrime (score 0.11) stays the current goal for 1-6 h with no plan, and the body stands still (`k20/courier_13695 d21 12:27-18:15`).
   - *Cause:* `systems/plan.rs::enqueue` orders `plan_queue` by urgency alone, under `plan_budget_per_tick`.
   - *Fix:* add the ticks waited to the sort key, or cool a goal that has been queued longer than an hour.
4. **Leisure is a far-off corner** `[feature]`, with a `[bug]` inside it
   - *Evidence:* travel (34-41 h a week) doubles leisure (17-21 h). The spot is 97-99 min from home (`s43/builder_1410 d27 06:44`). Unwind re-picks a spot that was just empty, up to 5 times in a row (Dorian, Dex).
   - *Bug cause:* `leisure::unwind_plan` and `choice` keep no memory of an empty spot.
   - *Fix:* score down a spot found empty on the last visit, and prefer spots within about 30 min of home.
   - *Feature:* leisure near home: a stoop, the Block's roof, a screen at home, a bar near the Block.
5. **Jail warms enemies** `[bug]`
   - *Evidence:* the assault victim goes from -1.00 to -0.10 in 13 h, and 60+ edges move every 4 h (`s43/worker_736 d26 02:01-19:37`).
   - *Cause:* the jail branch of `systems/social.rs::colocation`. A gang member's pitch drifts positive with every co-held prisoner, Enemy edges included.
   - *Fix:* skip pairs with an Enemy or Rival edge or an open grudge, or drift those pairs negative.
6. **Old and sleeping workers pick fights** `[bug]`
   - *Evidence:* a 58-year-old and a 48-year-old are woken to Attack (Fight replaced Sleep, `s43/night_shift_1758 d29 16:18`, `k20/fabber_11872 d20 19:58`). A 65-year-old leaves her meal to beat a man (`s43/night_shift_2381 d26 11:41`). Two of the three end up jailed (10 days each).
   - *Cause:* the Fight considerations in `utility/goals.rs` (`fighting` Linear{0.5, 0.5} turns 0.22 skill into 0.61) have no term for age, frailty or the opponent's strength.
   - *Fix:* add a relative-strength consideration (own fighting plus kit against the target's, times frailty) and damp Fight for an agent who is asleep at home.
7. **The work is a counter** `[feature]` (cabbie, loader, foreman, courier). Suggested mechanisms:
   - Cabbie: a hail to drive.
   - Loader: a crate. The Yard's fee could be paid per lift, with a Haul between the Yard and a Warehouse.
   - Foreman: make him an actor in the Hire and LaidOff events (`systems/demography.rs` hire, `systems/wages.rs` layoff), with a premium and a Pick action at shift start.
   - Courier: a parcel in the inventory for a named recipient, not the Super's round. The round's event also mislabels Clubs and Noodle Bars as "Blocks".
8. **A cult member is sold the chrome that gets him expelled** `[bug]` (V3 #7)
   - *Evidence:* `s44/worker_friday_2350 d94 17:29`.
   - *Cause:* `systems/assets.rs::shop_choice` ignores the gang's creed; `systems/creeds.rs` expels after the install.
   - *Fix:* drop chrome offers when the buyer's gang creed forbids chrome.
9. **Ages** `[feature]` (V3 #4, unchanged). Labourers aged 71-74 load, fab, build, drive and fight. Suggested: retirement or age-gated physical trades, and a foreman chosen by skill or real seniority rather than `industry::foreman`'s lowest `hired_tick`.
10. **Parenthood is invisible** `[feature]`. A mother of a two-day-old is out on corners at 01:39 and stealing a bike at 05:48 (`s44/fabber_1089 d90-d92`). A newborn needs a carer at home, a cut in night outings and a cost on the parents' purse and sleep.
11. **Gossip is only crime** `[feature]`. Add work deeds to `gossip::post_deed`: wages unpaid, laid off, quit, hired by the foreman, the boss's cut, a birth.
12. **A household has no shared business** `[feature]`. Rhoswen quits her wife's Garage unpaid, inherits it, then leaves it for a Vat Tech job (`k20/cabbie_14247 d23-d24`). Kin-run businesses need a shared purse or an owner-operator mode.
13. **The gang stipend is not booked** `[bug]`, minor
    - *Evidence:* a jailed member's purse rises +4c a night with no Flow (`s43/worker_736 d28-d31 00:00`).
    - *Cause:* the daily stipend in `systems/gang.rs` writes `Wallet.coins` directly.
    - *Fix:* pay it through `ownership::pay(.., Flow::Stipend)`.

One more gap was found by reading the code but is not seen in the Full diaries: `social::propose_allowed` has no Sentence check, so a jailed agent can be married by an off-screen suitor through `lod::stat_flirt`.
