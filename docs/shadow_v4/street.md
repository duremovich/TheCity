## Day labourer (no candidate on any run, the _full runs included)

Cause: `shadow.rs` picks `day_labourer` from `by_role` plus pitches at `--start-day 00:00`.
- A day post is hired at dawn (`streetwork.rs::dawn_pick`, `corner_hour` 06:00), with `Job.until` = the shift's end + 2 h.
- `industry::end_day_posts` removes it the same evening.
- So no one holds one at midnight, and the trade has no pitch.

The trade does exist: Ivo Chrome is "hired as Day Labourer" twice (`k20/builder_20660 d23 06:00`, `d24 06:00`).
Fix: pick from the dawn-pick Hires of the last 7 days.

## Gaps, ranked by how much they distort a real life

1. **The jobless poor with one meal's coins stop earning** `[bug]`. Benedikt (70) is idle 65 h with 1c, Brenna 52 h with 6c, Marek 46 h, Wisp Volkov 36 h. `utility/goals.rs::already_satisfied` (the `GoalKind::Earn` arm, lines 139-152) says "nothing to plan" for a jobless agent who is not `broke` when `levers.dole_per_day == 0`. With no net, that is everyone holding one meal's price. *Fix:* with the dole at 0, keep Earn plannable below `SAVINGS_DAYS` x meal price or with rent due.
2. **A killing is pinned on someone asleep across town** `[bug]`. Vale Crane sleeps on a Civic street from `d95 14:40`, yet the Sump East killing of day 95 is bound to her: Fought/Won memories and 11 grudges (`k2b/street_worker_2033 d96 00:00`). `bind.rs::candidates_in` weighs any agent alive and unjailed that day by trace. It never excludes an embodied agent whose position at the hole's tick is known and elsewhere, and the JAILED flag misses a release the same day. *Fix:* skip a candidate who was not Statistical at `hole.tick` unless they stood in `hole.district` then; at least never bind a pinned pick.
3. **20k distances eat the day** `[bug]`. `law.rs::private_patrol_route`, the `clients.len() > 5` branch, takes 5 random clients city-wide (Vex: 88 h travel). `gang.rs::holes_up_at` beds a LieLow or on-watch member at the Hideout at any distance (Axel: 901-min walks). *Fix:* take the 5 clients nearest the office; skip `holes_up_at` when Home is nearer by more than a walk cap, as `life::hideout_bed` already does.
4. **Pitch openings pick the wrong people** `[bug]`. An Arasaka 6 exec works a solicitation pitch, and 3 of 4 street workers are 64-68. `streetwork.rs::idle_days`/`openings` treat any adult without a `Job` as idle, execs included, and the stake test favours savers. *Fix:* exclude `life::exec_corp(..).is_some()`, cap the opener's coins, and keep elders past `frail_from_years` off the street and rickshaw rows.
5. **The street worker has no client** `[feature]`. `vice.rs::street_payer` is a hash over district residents. Make the payer a co-located evening walker scoring a Visit, so the night has a face, a haggle, a robber, and a pimp who collects.
6. **A spouse strips her father-in-law's body** `[bug]`. In the first pass (`k2_s43_d26/camp_warden_1673 d30 01:09`), Elspeth stripped Nico Ostrowski, her wife's father, for 7c, 3 h before her wife carried him to the cemetery. `chrome.rs::close_to` checks only the looter's own Family/Spouse/Parent edges. *Fix:* also refuse kin of `world.spouse_of(agent)`, housemates, and a body the looter holds a `Grief` memory of.
7. **The pitch spot is the district's first free spot, not the nearest** `[bug]`. Vendors walk 47-168 min each way. `streetwork.rs::openings` uses `.find(|t| !used.contains(t))`. *Fix:* take the free spot nearest the home door, and re-site a pitch after its owner's eviction.
8. **A worker goes hungry on shift with coins in hand** `[bug]`. Nikolai works with hunger at 0.11 and 15c, 26 h after his last meal. The `GoalKind::Work` considerations in `utility/goals.rs` have no hunger term. *Fix:* add a "fed" consideration (Unwind has one) so a hungry worker takes a meal break.
9. **Spouses live apart and never meet** `[feature]`. In all 5 of my picks with a spouse who lives apart, news of the spouse travels by gossip and the couple never meets: Hilde, Joss, Marek, Brenna, Lorcan. Add a weekly "see my spouse" target weighted by affinity, a visit after hearing the spouse was hurt, and a move-in when one spouse is housed (`social::marry` only moves in at the wedding, and only if there is room).
10. **Sickness has no behaviour** `[feature]`. Malnutrition 0.57-0.68 and toxicity 0.85-0.92 change nothing: about 5-6 meals a week, flat meters, no care, 1 sick day in 12 picks. Let the sick eat better when they can, rest, seek a Clinic or Mission, and be noticed by others.
11. **The camp warden's work is a counter** `[feature]`. Name 2-3 children per shift, with headcount, a beating, an escape, or a parent's bribe.
12. **Shadow pick bugs** `[bug]`, in `shadow.rs` pick arms:
    - `day_labourer` can never match at midnight (fix above).
    - `sick` takes cancer at stage 0.00. *Fix:* require diagnosed, or stage >= 0.3.
    - `vendor`/`street_worker` take a pitch the same midnight closes. *Fix:* filter `!has::<Job>` and `!has::<Sentence>`.
13. **No elder is taken in or visited** `[feature]`. V3 gap 10 is unchanged. A friend or kin edge above +0.5 should bring food or a bed when the elder is broke.
14. **A StakeOut runs in 2-minute loops** `[bug]`, minor: 19 StakeOuts on one target in a night (`k2a/sick_1052 d32 03:09`). The locate contract's StakeOut ends at once and replans. *Fix:* give StakeOut a minimum duration (30-60 min) while the target is unsighted.
