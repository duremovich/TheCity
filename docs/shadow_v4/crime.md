# Shadow V4: the crime trades

Arms dealer, forger, fixer, gun, buyer, pimp; and the black doc, which has no candidate on any run. Three runs, 7 days each, pinned at Full LOD: `k2_s43_d26_full` (2k, seed 43), `k2_s44_d90_full` (2k, seed 44, mature) and `k20_s42_d20` (20k, seed 42). That's 26 diaries. Citations are `run/file dDD HH:MM`, with the runs shortened to `s43f`, `s44f` and `k20`. The first 2k runs, which overflowed `max_full`, are not used.

**Short version.** The crime trades are mostly labels pinned on ordinary lives. The **gun** is the one crime trade that works as a life: stake-outs, sold sightings, three corps hunting a gang boss. Everywhere else the trade happens without a body. Papers sell at midnight to buyers nobody meets, five at a time. The fixer's cut comes in while the fixer is at a vat farm. A tag pays 261c while the gun rests. The pimp's cut goes to the gang. Across 35 dealer-days the arms dealers sold no weapons.

## Black doc: absent, and the code says it can't appear

`gray::build_clinics` needs all of the following:
- a gang under Harvest (or a typed scav gang), not Purist;
- **treasury >= found_cost 500 + `black_clinic_spare` 200 = 700c**;
- a vacant Lot in a district the gang holds.

The richest gang treasury in all the diary headers is 180c (the Drowned Choir on seed 44); the others run 0-55c. Even with a clinic, its posts hire only struck-off docs (`gray::hire_ok`), and a Ripperdoc is struck off at `p_struck_off` 0.002 × (1 - lawfulness) a day, so about one a quarter on 2k. Two gates stack on a treasury the economy never reaches. V3 gave the same diagnosis, and it's unchanged.

## 2k vs 20k, day 26 vs day 90

- **Jail.** On 2k the crime picks spend 817 of 2,352 hours inside (35 %). On 20k they spend 10 of 2,352 (<1 %). City-wide the 2k diaries average 28-30 jail hours each, against 8.7 on 20k. Every one of those hours is backed by a `[law] JAILED` line. The cause is the arrest rate, not the LOD (gap 11).
- **Gray market shape.** On 2k it is one corp-owned Fixer (#531) holding every forger and fixer in two seeds. On 20k it is many agent-owned Fixers run by absentee owners with day jobs.
- **Money.** The 20k tag payouts (261-312c) make a pensioner the richest pick in the set. The best 2k forger out-earns the 20k forgers (192c vs 64-96c a week).
- **Day 90.** Institutions are forming (a jailed pimp incorporates, the Drowned Choir holds 180c and draws forgers in), and more time is spent inside (s44f: Bertram 168 h, Gwendolyn 147, Percival 136).

## Gaps, ranked

1. **Arms dealers sell no arms** `[bug]` + `[feature]`. There were 0 weapon sales in 35 dealer-days.
   - The picker tests `stock_at` non-empty (`shadow.rs::candidates` "arms_dealer"), but a sale needs `weapons::surplus` above 0.3 × members. *Fix:* pick on `!surplus(gang).is_empty()` or a `Flow::Arms` in 7 days.
   - Surplus never forms: crates are paid only above `crate_reserve_days` of stipends, from treasuries of 0-180c.
   - Wants exclude gang members and need list + 20 coins.
   - Dealers sell 2 stims in 14 shifts and dose themselves (V2 #14).
   - *Feature:* let a gang buy a crate on credit from its syndicate and sell down the debt, and consign the stock to the dealer.
2. **The Fixer is a ledger, not a person** `[feature]`. Owners never visit, hired fixers broker nothing, and papers sell at midnight to strangers (five in one tick).
   - A post, a take and a paper sale should be a client's walk to an open Fixer during a fixer's shift. `papers_daily` becomes a Papers want the Shop goal fills there.
   - The fixer meets buyer and taker (edges, first-hand `Hired`).
   - The owner collects in person or hires one.
3. **A Tag runs off-screen for a pinned gun** `[bug]`. `contracts.rs::render_for` returns Ledger for `Tag | Vote` before it checks `party_shown`, so a Full-LOD taker is paid 261c while resting. *Fix:* fall through to `party_shown` for Tag (StakeOut > Plant as a live step). Also print tag vs hit prices as a calibration finding.
4. **Rest replaces sleep** `[bug]`. In `exec/actions.rs`, Rest's completion adds +0.1 energy per 90 min, which outpaces decay, so an agent looping Idle>Rest never needs Sleep. Zelda goes 4 days at energy 0.85-0.97 with no sleep. *Fix:* cap Rest's gain (e.g. no gain above 0.6, or +0.03) so sleep stays the only full restore.
5. **A Locate taker who works is not paid** `[bug]`. In `contracts.rs::fulfil`, the Locate branch refunds the remaining escrow to the buyer once anyone's sightings hit the cap. Hollis staked out for 3 days and got 0c. *Fix:* on fulfil, pay the taker what's left of the price after the sightings and the cut, or count only the taker's and crew's sightings once Taken.
6. **The pimp is a title** `[feature]`. There's no edge, presence or purse link to the street workers.
   - A nightly Collect step at his pitches puts the cut in his purse, and he passes a share up.
   - Protection becomes a presence roll: within N tiles during pitch hours.
   - The worker gets a Boss edge, and a pimp who never shows loses the pitch.
7. **The marked man doesn't react; the buyer doesn't escalate** `[bug]`. In `grudges.rs::on_learn`, a rumour of a hit on yourself weighs `deed_sev.hired` 0.7 × `rel_w.own` 0.6 × conf 0.6 = 0.25, below `grudge_min` 0.35. *Fix:* a `Hired` deed whose object is the holder skips `grudge_min` and raises the safety need (Flee or a Guard post). *Feature:* a refunded buyer with an unsettled grudge of 0.5 or more re-posts or hunts.
8. **A Purist or cult member buys chrome and is expelled for it** `[bug]`. `assets.rs::chrome_max_tier` returns 3 for any gang member, with no creed check. *Fix:* cap at the gang creed's tolerance (`creeds` row) for members of a Purist or cult gang. This is V3 #7 generalised.
9. **The secure quitter is rehired the same midnight** `[bug]`. Nyx: `fixes.rs` secure quit, then `demography.rs` candidates filter only `life::quit_blocks`, which covers just the employer they left. *Fix:* a "no need of the wage" quit blocks every hire for `quit_rehire_days`, or skip candidates at `wealth >= 1.0`.
10. **The black clinic is unreachable** `[bug]`. `gray::build_clinics` needs 700c against treasuries of at most 180c, and staffs only struck-off docs. *Fix:* make the spare relative (0, or a share of found cost) and let a struck-off doc open a back-room pitch in a Block with no clinic.
11. **Crime is rarely jailed at 20k** `[bug]`, cause not traced. Re-measured on the `_full` runs, and it is not an LOD artefact.
   - The time-use "jail" column is set only by a `Sentence` component (`shadow.rs::activity`). Every diary with jail hours in all five runs has a matching `[law] JAILED` line.
   - Crime-trade picks: 817 of 2,352 h inside on 2k against 10 of 2,352 on 20k (35 % vs <1 %). City-wide: 30 and 27.5 jail h per diary (s43f, s44f) against 8.7 (k20).
   - Witnessed crimes per diary are about the same (5.6, 5.0, 5.1). Distinct reports drop from 0.32-0.40 to 0.18 per diary, and arrests from 0.58-0.62 to 0.30. 20k turns a witnessed crime into an arrest at about half the 2k rate.
   - Check the per-capita caps (R3) and the report and arrest paths in `law.rs` against the 20k guard and Precinct count.
12. **A homeless Dreg with knowledge 0.00 is hired as a Teacher** `[bug]`. That's `s43f/arms_dealer_2474 d28 00:00`. The foreman's pick (`demography.rs` candidates) doesn't gate on the row's `skill`. *Fix:* a floor on the trade's skill for skilled rows (teacher, ripperdoc, clerk).
13a. **The diary blames a pin overflow on sentencing** `[bug]`. In `shadow.rs`, the LOD-change note adds "(sentencing sets Coarse until the next hourly assignment re-pins it)" to every Full -> Coarse drop of a pinned pick, whether or not the agent holds a `Sentence`. The overflowed first 2k runs show it with no arrest at all (`k2_s43_d26/arms_dealer_2501 d26 14:00`), and it still fires 79 times in the `_full` and k20 diaries. *Fix:* print the sentencing note only when `jailed` is true; otherwise print "(over `[lod] max_full`: pin not honoured)". Jail hours are unaffected (see gap 11).
13. **The diaries' money section misreads flows** `[bug]`. `shadow.rs::flow_kind` buckets by step: Gray at midnight reads "daily pass", a mid-shift purchase reads "wage". *Fix:* bucket by the `Flow` kind.
14. **The pimp picker takes a jailed pimp** `[bug]`. `shadow.rs` "pimp" reads `vice.pimps` raw; Bertram spent all 168 h inside. *Fix:* reuse `pimp_of`'s `!has::<Sentence>` filter.
15. **An accident has no aftermath** `[feature]`. A witnessed Manslaughter gets no report in 7 days, and the driver chats with the witness. *Feature:* a guilt memory and a Flee or Hide reaction for the doer, plus vehicle seizure on report.

Minor, not ranked:
- `gray::forger_near` doesn't exclude the buyer, so a wanted forger could buy papers from himself.
- The news names two different payers for one old kill (`s44f/pimp_790 d91 00:00` Kessler, `d96 00:00` Habitat).

## Verdicts

- **Gun:** alive (hunts, extortion, three buyers on one boss), apart from the invisible tag and a boss who never sleeps.
- **Forger:** thin. The law gives the 2k forgers a life; the trade itself is a midnight deposit.
- **Buyer:** thin. One decision before the window and a memory after it.
- **Arms dealer:** not alive as a trade. The people carrying the label are squatters, sweepers and prisoners.
- **Fixer:** not alive. Owners are absentee rentiers; hired fixers are clerks.
- **Pimp:** not alive. The title runs no business.
- **Black doc:** absent, and unreachable by construction.
