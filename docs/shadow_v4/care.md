Ripperdoc, orderly, teacher, super: 24 diaries, two per archetype per run. Runs: `k2_s43_d26_full` (2k, seed 43, d26-33), `k2_s44_d90_full` (2k, seed 44, d90-97) and `k20_s42_d20` (20k, seed 42, d20-27). Below they are s43, s44 and k20. ## Gaps, ranked by how much they distort a life

1. **Care and civic work has no counterpart** `[feature]`. There is no patient, orderly task, pupil or tenant. Proposed:
   - The Clinic's shift drains a queue built from the sources that already exist: Shop chrome and `Install` buyers, the wounded carried in (`wounded.rs`), the sick. Each visit is a two-party Treat with a fee cut and a `Patient`/`Doctor` edge. The orderly gets the carry-in and the triage.
   - The School gets a roster of the district's children.
   - The super's round stops at household doors: collect rent, warn arrears (the super serves the eviction), and log a repair against the Block's condition.
2. **The robbed fight back and serve 10 days** `[bug]`. Agathe (48, fighting 0.15) and Wire (52, fighting 0.20) each attack the person who robbed them minutes or hours before, and each gets 10 days for Assault (s43 d27 09:18, d27 19:12). Dagny, Emeric and Leopold also attack, so 5 of 24 picks. *Cause:* in `utility/goals.rs`, `GoalKind::Fight` adds a flat +0.2 when an enemy who robbed you is in reach. That bonus does not scale with courage or skill, so it beats ReportCrime (0.43 vs 0.39 for Agathe). *Fix:*
   - scale the bonus by courage × fighting;
   - for a timid, unskilled victim, make Flee or ReportCrime the answer;
   - a law-side "provoked" discount for an assault on one's own robber within a day.
3. **A 1c fare is the city's top sentence** `[bug]`. Fare Evasion leads the sentences in all three runs (42, against 33 for Assault). Odile is arrested at her desk (k20 d22 16:50), and Tristan in a bar 22 h after the dodge (s44 d92 05:22). Garrick and Sebastian each lose 10 h. *Cause:* `law.rs::jail_suspect` uses `dodge_fine` only when the Precinct is full, so otherwise a report means an arrest anywhere, any time later. *Fix:* fine Fare Evasion (and Vagrancy) whenever the suspect can pay, and jail only for an unpaid fine.
4. **Spouses rarely meet** `[feature]`. 14 of 17. Home chats work when both are in. Still needed: a spouse at home in the evening, a weekly "see someone I love" goal, and the spot pick weighted toward loved contacts.
5. **Extortion picks a Home, not a victim, so friends rob friends** `[bug]`. *Cause:* `gang.rs::extort_target` / `gang_work_target` choose a Home by guard cover and claim, and `extort` takes from every occupant whatever its edge to the actor. *Fix:* skip a Home where the actor has a Friend, Spouse or kin, or leave those occupants out of the take.
6. **A Chat survives a robbery by its partner** `[bug]`. *Cause:* the `ActionKind::Chat` arm in `exec/actions.rs` checks only proximity. *Fix:* fail it when the edge falls to `rival_threshold` or below, and write no `Socialised` memory.
7. **Gang leadership goes to loyalty, not tenure** `[bug]`. Liesl joins d22 and leads d25; Rex joins d27 and leads d30. *Cause:* `gang.rs::leader_scores` scores `loyalty + days/100`, so 100 days in the gang count as much as one point of loyalty. *Fix:* weight tenure at about days/20, or require a minimum number of days for the acting chair.
8. **Owners do not run what they own** `[feature]`. Rex owns a Clinic, is evicted and clerks at a Market. Liesl tends bar. Hana's bar payroll comes out of her orderly wage. *Proposed:*
   - a jobless owner takes the post at their own business;
   - the owner is paid from the business's profit before payroll comes out of their purse;
   - `gang::eligible` refuses a cult row to Clinic owners and staff.
9. **A taken child is a grudge, not a loss** `[feature]`. Cass's child goes to the Work Camp, and Cass's only response is a GrudgeFormed. *Proposed:* a parent's goal to visit, buy out or break out a child from a Camp, weighted by the kin edge. A parent who never sees a child is a neglect signal the service can read, so the story has a cause.
10. **No tied housing for supers** `[feature]`. Bex is evicted by her own landlord-employer and keeps the post from the street. Jinx is rent short with his employer on d28. *Proposed:* a super lives rent-free in a Block on the round.
11. **A trade is not a profession** `[feature]`. A 19-year-old teaches. A Clinic owner is hired as a Market Clerk by "the foreman". *Proposed:* a `Profession` memory of the last trade that hiring prefers, and age bands on `ages = true` trades (teacher from about 25).
12. **Grocery runs from bed at night** `[bug]`. There are 9 market trips between 00:00 and 05:00. 3 of them interrupt sleep: k20/super_21967 d22 02:07, k20/teacher_12815 d23 01:00, s43/orderly_2347 d29 03:07. All are walks of about 60 min. *Cause:* the grocery branch of `utility/goals.rs` (`Eat`, "pantry under a shift's meals", 0.5) is damped only past a *work night's* bedtime. *Fix:* zero it between 23:00 and 06:00, or while Sleep is the running goal.
13. **Empty hang-outs, hour after hour** `[feature]`. 120 of 369 hang-outs found nobody there. *Proposed:* leave after one empty hour for a spot where a known contact is now.
14. **The super's round as travel** `[feature]`. 63-79 h a week on 6-Block rounds. *Proposed:* the 3-4 nearest Blocks, or count the walk as work once the round has door visits (gap 1).
15. **Earn held all afternoon on a rest day, with no plan** `[bug]`, cause unconfirmed. Examples: s43/orderly_2347 d27 14:34-18:04 and s43/teacher_2035 d27 15:01-18:01. Both release at the end of the shift the agent is off. *Suspect:* Earn's plan waits on the shift clock, which ignores the rest day. Probe `exec::routine` / the Earn plan against `Job` rest days.
