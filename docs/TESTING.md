# Testing

2026-10-09 (Dylan): "The most important thing is emergent story. For that we need people that appear to act like people." The suite was rebuilt around that: a fast core that catches a *broken* city, a behaviour tier that judges whether people *act like people*, and a short list of god scenarios. Everything else that used to be asserted (calibration bands, multi-seed trajectories, numbers pinned to an older city) is gone.

**The doctrine, in three lines:**

1. Asserts catch broken: a panic, nondeterminism, a coin made or lost, a collapse, a mechanism that never fires.
2. Behaviour judges alive: the archetypes' diaries and the person probes are the primary judge.
3. Numbers are reports, not gates: `tools/analyze_run.py` prints them from `citysim-cli run --report`.

## When to run what

- **Every change** (the per-change loop, ~1.5 min after compile):
  ```
  cargo fmt --all -- --check
  cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings
  cargo test --workspace --release
  ```
- **Every merge** (the loop plus the year, behaviour and god, ~4 min more; run them one at a time, each spawns its own threads):
  ```
  cargo test --release -p citysim --test core -- --ignored core_year --nocapture
  cargo test --release -p citysim-cli -- --ignored behaviour --nocapture   # or: citysim-cli shadow --assert
  cargo test --release -p citysim --test god --test god_corps --test god_districts -- --ignored --nocapture
  ```
- **Every milestone** (or nightly): the merge set, the parity test, and a human (or model critic) read of the diaries:
  ```
  cargo test --release -p citysim --test lod -- --ignored --nocapture
  cargo test --release -p citysim --test core -- --ignored core_20k --nocapture   # the 20k profile, alone (~5 min)
  citysim-cli shadow --assert --out shadow_out     # keeps the diaries under shadow_out/d19, shadow_out/d90
  ```
  plus a shadow pass in the style of `docs/SHADOW_V2.md` (critics read one diary per archetype and write what would make it real). That read is the final judge of "alive"; the bounds only catch regressions between reads.
- **Profiling** (Scale S1, `citysim/src/prof.rs`): build with `--features prof` and `run` prints a `PROF` line per timed site (seconds, share of the tick, calls) at the end; `--prof-from DAY` zeroes it at that day's start, so the profile is the run's tail. Off (the default build) the timers compile to nothing. A scaled city for measurement: `run --population N --scaled [--rph R] [--map FILE]` (`Config::scaled_to`).
  ```
  cargo build --release -p citysim-cli --features prof
  target/release/citysim-cli run --days 30 --report --population 20000 --scaled --rph 50 --map <map> --prof-from 25 > run.csv 2> prof.txt
  ```
- **Big-map paths** (Scale S3a): `cargo test --release -p citysim --test big_island -- --ignored --nocapture` runs the synthetic 3,000 × 1,875 island (`CITYSIM_BENCH_POP`, `_DAYS`, `_FULL` for the population, days and `max_full`) and prints ticks/s, the path queries' latency (p50/p90/p99, Manhattan-only against the landmarks), the pathing state's bytes and the peak working set; `paths.rs::measure_flow_field_vs_big_path` (ignored) prints a flow field's build against an A* query by map size.

## The core tier

`core_sanity` runs the shipped config (`Config::load()`) on seeds 42, 43 and 44 for 120 days, each in its own thread, and walks every event by id cursor once an hour into one collector per seed. It fails on:

- **the coin identity** (`econ::identity`: `total_coins + Σ outside treasuries − minted`) moving on any day;
- **escrow** (M16a): `escrow_leak` (Σ `Contract.escrow` − `World::escrow_held`) non-zero on any day (also judged per window in `core_year`);
- **collapse**: population outside 1,333..=2,667 on any day; starvation over 200 in 120 days; Assault + Murder events over 42.7 a day; the Treasury below 0 from day 30; the Jail over capacity; every Market empty three days running; fewer than 4 seeded corps alive at the end; any prisoner held at the Statistical tier starving;
- **a mechanism that never fired** on any of the three seeds (the list below);
- **hunger as sickness** (on with the flip, always on since P10): starvation over 20 per 120 days (Dylan: a broken free-food floor), and five more bullets (a synth meal, a bin meal, a bin shakedown, a malnourished agent, a sick day). J25 counts adults sleeping on the street (Dylan, 2026-10-10: a squat, a cot or a Hotel bed is a rung); the Dregs and their split print beside it;
- **ticks/s** under 4,000 on seed 42 (release only). The core binary holds one non-ignored test and cargo runs test binaries one at a time, so the seed runs alone but for its two sibling threads (it reads ~6,000 there; ~10,700 truly alone and idle).

**The 20k profile** (`core_20k`, Scale S4, docs/SCALE_20K.md § S4): the same bounds per capita, the counts (population range, starvation, assaults a day, corps alive) times `Config::per_capita` (10 at 20,000; 1 in the shipped city), the Jail's capacity read from the fitted world. Employment is asserted as a **collapse floor, not a calibration band**: employed >= 0.40 of free adults from day 30 (S3b's collapsed 20k city read 0.17). J24's 0.5 is printed beside it as a `FINDING` (the 20k city reads ~0.50 on day 30, the 2,000 city ~0.53: a calibration gap, written up in SCALE_20K.md). Determinism: seed 42 runs twice in parallel threads and the closing save hashes must match. Ticks/s is printed against `TPS_FLOOR_20K` (140: 189 measured with the two runs in parallel after S4 perf, less ~25 %), never asserted (the box is noisy). The mechanism bullets are `core_sanity`'s, not repeated here. **M17 phase 1**: the profile runs on an island of Ambient flats (`[ambient] flats_per_slot` 50 on gen20k) and asserts an Ambient sale (`ambient_food_bought + ambient_visits_made > 0`) on every day from day 2, the book's one existence bullet (the 2k city has no flats); the book's day (units and visits wanted and bought, coins paid, their share of wages) prints as `FINDING` lines.

**The Ambient tier** (`citysim/tests/ambient.rs`, Scale S5, docs/SCALE_20K.md § S5; unit tier, ~7 s): the shipped city has no Ambient flats (no columns, nothing in the save); a flat gives the same person in any order and a day later; a materialised household is housed, Statistical and paid from the World (the coin identity holds); the labour pool's daily cap; the midnight job search hires from the pool when the city is short; immigration draws Ambient households before the map edge; a save resumes byte for byte; and the parity-style check: a cohort materialised on day 20 is printed against the born-simulated adults of the same class on day 20 and day 30 (needs, mood, wallet median, contacts median, employed share), loosely asserted (a broken materialisation, not a calibration).

The bounds are the v1 sanity trio scaled to 2,000 residents (M10 plan D38), unchanged since M8. `core_year` runs the same bounds over each 30-day window of a 365-day seed-42 run, the Assault bound at 1.5 x (a 30-day window is noisier than 120 days; seed 42 peaks at 36.3).

The mechanism bullets (61, one per milestone's core mechanism, measured on 42-47; each fired on 4-6 of the 6 seeds): a Marriage, a GangJoin, a Birth, a Burial (M5, M6); a raid resolved, a Home flipped (M8); a Jailbreak, a day in Crackdown (M9); a hole bound (M10); an eviction, an NPC founding, a hostile takeover, a Strike (M11); a Riot, a gang Split, a Squat (M12); a Crash, a chrome Install, a Dealing report (M13); a run, a Data sale, a Flatline (M14); a Feed Story, a Hunt, a Vendetta, a Purist expulsion (M15); a World export, a Collect, a Bout, a gang front, a Mission meal (L2, the Real economy); a BountyPaid, a city guard on the take (M16a phase 3); a Guard fulfilled, a Locate fulfilled, a Fixer in business (open, a record brokered there within 7 days), a Reneged (M16a phase 5, each on 6 of 6; the Hit chain fired on 0 of 6 and is unit-tested: `core.rs`'s comment lists the tests); a protection paid, a blackmail made, a debt called in, a report withdrawn under a threat, a coerced job, a fake job, a Parley (M16b phase 1, leverage; the rare ones, a lapse, a Fraud filed, a poach by threat, a Persuade, a coerced job done, are printed and unit-tested in `leverage.rs`); an Abduct posted, a Steal fulfilled, a heist run, a Spin posted (M16b phase 2; a hostage taken, a Rescue, an Escort, a Deliver fulfilled, a fabrication planted or exposed are printed and unit-tested in `captives.rs`); a downed agent, a bleed-out, a stabilisation, a rescue by kin, a downed agent finished off, a Trauma subscription (M16b phase 3; a rescue by a gang-mate, a Trauma call and a TraumaSave are printed and unit-tested in `wounded.rs`); a board vote, a campaign move, a VoteSwung, a tag planted, a tag found, a scan, a day a faction is distracted (M16b phase 4; a Purist Patrol scan, a board Persuade and a Vote record are unit-tested in `tags.rs` and `governance.rs`, the Patrol printed; and the run fails on a day a faction's alertness ÷ pool sits under `alert_floor`). M17 phase 2: a day with a remittance home and a Subsidised (each on 6 of 6; the parents' failure flag, the Embargo, the tariff and the pre-M17 migration are unit-tested in `parents.rs`). M17 phase 3: an OutsidersArrived, a BrothersArrived and a StateGrant (6 of 6 each; a Retake, a buy-back, a FoundBranch, a refound, a tour's end and a settling are unit-tested in `bodies.rs`, a ParentFounded and an Abandoned printed). With `[transit] enabled` (the shipped default since transit phase 3) two more, `transit_mechanisms`: an El ride and a fare paid to the city (every day on 42-44), and the ride sanity fails the run: a filed ride with its body on none of its legs, or a body aboard past its arrival.

### Adding a mechanism-existence bullet

1. Run `core_sanity --nocapture`: it prints every event kind's count and every mark per seed.
2. If the mechanism is an event kind, add `("a Thing", k(EventKind::Thing))` to `mechanisms()` in `core.rs` with the 42-44 counts in a comment. If it needs more than a kind (an event's text, a day-end state, a `DayRow` column), add a mark in `run_seed` (`Some("my mark")` in the event match, or `day_mark(...)` at the day's end) and `m("my mark")`.
3. **Rare mechanisms get a unit test instead.** If it fires on 2 or fewer of 6 seeds (42-47) in 120 days, do not add a bullet (it will flip with every unrelated change). Write a unit test in a seeded world that sets up the preconditions and calls the system directly. Examples: `tech.rs::test_research_held_builds_a_lab_in_the_focus` (a Lab under Research, 2 of 6 seeds), `camp.rs::test_unfed_child_taken_at_take_days_before_death` (a child taken, 0 of 6), the contract posting tests in `contracts.rs` (1 of 6). A bullet that turns out rare is demoted the same way and keeps its per-seed counts as a printed `FINDING` line (`core.rs::printed_mechanisms`): a Riot and a gang front (hunger as sickness P5i, 2026-10-10: both read [0, 0, 0] on seeds 42-44 under a one-step butterfly with the change off, and fired under other butterflies with it on), now `districts.rs::test_riot_fires_at_streak_and_picks_target` (the Riot event at the streak, none a midnight short) and `leisure.rs::test_front_built_under_expand_in_held_district` with its negative `test_no_front_without_expand_or_treasury`; and a Jailbreak (the flipped city after the recalibration: [0, 0, 0] on 42-44, [0, 1, 1] under a butterfly), now `factions.rs::test_breach_with_no_guards_frees_the_boss_first_and_reopens_the_warrant` with its negatives `test_breach_with_nobody_inside_fizzles` and `test_breach_against_three_guards_fails_and_shocks`. And a day in Crackdown (the Hit market P2, 2026-10-11: days held over 42-47 [4, 9, 0, 1, 0, 0] on the flipped main, [0, 0, 0, 4, 6, 0] with P2), now `law_faction.rs::test_posture_patrol_at_rest_and_crackdown_under_pressure`. And an organic Hit fulfilled (the Hit market P4, 2026-10-11: `hm_hits_done` over 42-47 [1, 1, 1, 0, 1, 0] on `fe60d37`, [1, 1, 1, 0, 0, 0] with P4's D8 fix, 2 of 6 before the P2 fix round; one a seed at most), never a bullet: `hitmarket.rs` (the price, the pooled deposit, credit, stiffing), `person.rs::probe_widow_commissions_a_hit_at_the_fixer` and `god_kill_ten_fathers_day_10` carry it. `tools/analyze_run.py`'s "Hit market" section prints the organic records' story list from an events file.

Never add a band. If a number matters, print it in `run --report` and read it with `tools/analyze_run.py`.

## The behaviour tier

### `shadow --assert`

`citysim-cli shadow --assert [--out DIR]` (or `test_behaviour_tier`) runs the V2 shadow pass's pinned windows, 7 days from day 19 (a Friday) with every archetype and 7 days from day 90 with the gang archetypes, on seeds 42, 43 and 44. Each window's world is run to its start day once per seed; then **each archetype gets its own clone of it** with up to 30 of its free candidates pinned at Full (every candidate in a small pool), all in parallel threads. A row pools the archetype's agents over the three seeds:

- a **share** (`worked`, `paid/wkd`, `waged_wd`, `bouts_at`) is pooled over every shift or bout, and judged only over 20 or more of them;
- every other metric is a **per-agent rate, averaged as a 10 % trimmed mean** over the agents alive a whole day of the window (one agent's 6 h commute or week at energy 0 does not carry a row; a regression most of them share does);
- a row is judged only over 5 or more agents (never a smaller sample than the old 5 picks); under a floor it is a SKIP, not a failure.

The first 5 of each archetype's sample write their diaries (`--out DIR`: `d19/`, `d90/` for seed 42, `s43_d19/` and so on): the diaries are the human-readable judge and are not what the numbers are read from. Some archetypes are diaries only, outside the windows and `CHECKS`: `fixer`, `gun`, `sick` and `buyer` (the Hit market P4: a want's holder or the placing agent of an organic Hit or Beat live or posted in the last 7 days; `shadow --pick buyer`, or `--agent <id>` from `hit_probe::hit_market_report`'s story lines, which print each record's buyer and target indices). The table prints the pooled row and each seed's row (`cook@43`) so a failure shows which city carried it.

The metrics:

| metric | what |
|---|---|
| `walk_h/d` | travel-class hours a day |
| `sleep_h/d` | sleep-class hours a day (printed) |
| `longest_h` | the mean over agent-nights (noon to noon) of the night's longest sleep block |
| `e0_h/wk` | hours at energy 0 per agent-week |
| `work_h/d` | work-class hours a day |
| `worked` | whole workday shifts (free) with half the shift at work-class steps (printed) |
| `paid/wkd` | worked shifts paid a wage by two hours after the end |
| `waged_wd` | whole workday shifts (free) with a wage |
| `hang/wk` | HangOut steps per agent-week (printed) |
| `known/wk` | distinct known contacts met at a HangOut per agent-week |
| `refund/wk` | purchases refunded (a started buy undone) per agent-week |
| `abort/d` | Work goals dropped before a single work-class minute (a commute turned back) per agent-day |
| `bouts_at` | bouts with the fighter inside the pit, of the bouts naming her |
| `starve_h/wk` | free hours at hunger < `starving_hunger` per agent-week (printed, no bound yet) |
| `synth%` | synth and Mission meals of all meals, pooled (printed) |
| `dive/wk` | bin meals per agent-week (printed) |

**The bounds are calibrated on the noise, not on one reading** (last re-calibrated 2026-10-11 on `a6887f1`, the shipped `stat_violence_mult` 1.0 and `max_coarse` 150 at the centre). `CHECKS` in `shadow.rs` holds each row's bound and the range the row read on a calibration ensemble: the tier on main and on seven config butterflies (`[lod] stat_violence_mult` 0.98, 0.99, 1.01, 1.02, 1.03 and `max_coarse` 149, 151 in a copy of `assets/` passed as `CITYSIM_ASSETS`: the same rules, a city that diverges from day 1, so every pick is someone else's week). Each bound sits a cushion outside that range: walking + max(1 h, 15 %), the longest nightly block − 0.5 h, energy 0 + 2 h a week, known contacts met × 0.6, commute aborts + 0.05 a day, paid of worked and waged workdays − 0.1, the reporter's work hours × 0.5; refunds ≤ 1 a week and the fighter in the pit for ≥ 3 bouts in 4 are absolute. A FAIL line prints the calibration range beside the value. The d90 runner rows are not judged (3-10 agents over three seeds, an archetype defined by a run in the last 7 days: they moved more under the butterflies than any injected regression moved anything). They catch a regression in "acts like a person"; they are not targets.

Why (measured 2026-10-09, `d11e397`; the numbers are in the commit message): the 5-pick tier, its bounds re-derived on main by its own rules, flipped 1-4 rows on every one of the seven butterflies (as shipped, with its accumulated margins, 0-3 on six of seven) and 2-6 on the session's earlier commits, every flip a pick change or one city's gang or Law trajectory (the guards patrol or stand post as a city, the Purists of one seed sleep 2.5 h a day). Three causes: five people are a sample of five; one seed is one gang trajectory; and the archetypes shared one world, so a pick change in one moved the city under all the others. The pooled tier, calibrated on main and four butterflies, flipped no row on three held-out butterflies or on `be7e4d3` (before the trades), and one on `7a0f003` (before Jobs P2's 100 Lots: the ripperdocs walk 5.6 h a day on all three seeds there against 3.6-4.5 today, a real change). On M16a phase 4 (`2935f18`) the 5-pick tier failed two d90 rows on a jailed runner pick; the pooled tier passes. Injected regressions: a role that never chooses Eat (the sweepers) fails `KnownMetWeek` (3.2 against a range of 11.7-14.0, all three seeds); breaking the commute latch (L2 shadow fix 1) fails d90 homeless `CommuteAbortsDay` (0.11 against 0.00-0.03); undoing the committed Sleep (fix 3) is not caught (the longest block drops ~0.2 h on three archetypes, inside the noise; the 5-pick tier caught the Eat one on its sweeper rows, missed the latch, and failed the Sleep one only on two d90 gang-leader rows that also flip on butterflies).

**When the tier fails**: read the row's per-seed lines, then the diaries (`--out`). If the city really changed and the change is wanted, re-calibrate: run `shadow --assert` on the new main and on the seven butterflies (eight runs of ~1.5 min), take each row's lowest and highest pooled value over the eight, apply the cushions above (a Min rounded down and a Max up, to 0.01 for aborts and shares, else 0.1), and record the old and new ranges in the commit message. Re-calibrate from one run only when a single row moved for a known reason, widening that row's range by the new reading. A stat table regeneration or a recalibration that does not change how people behave should pass without one; that is the point.

### Person probes

`citysim/tests/person.rs`: the shipped seed-42 city, one agent pinned at Full LOD, one stimulus, and an assert on what a person would do. The always-on probes (all pass; the Jobs probes that need the wages switches are `#[ignore]`d until the flip):

- hungry, broke, an empty larder at 15:00: paid at 18:00, buys food before midnight's rent;
- married, across town at 20:00, tired, no Hotel bed affordable (2026-10-11: an affordable away Hotel is L2's designed bed): at home at 03:00;
- an Enemy standing at the nearer HangOut spot: the other spot;
- at the counter at 17:45, a little hungry: stays to 18:00 and is paid;
- an immigrant with no coins and no food: eats or is hired within five days, alive;
- a guard starting a twelve-hour shift hungry: eats on shift, never at hunger 0;
- a Night Porter on shift at 02:00 at the Hotel, paid by 08:00;
- M16a: a gun alone on a Fixer's books never takes a Hit on his own wife (the control: he takes the same Hit on a stranger);
- M16a: the buyer of a fulfilled Hit nobody else knew of is never charged before somebody holds the `Hired` deed (30 days);
- M16a: a broke jobless hand with a skill a Fixer can sell networks at the Fixer within a week.
- The Hit market (the H7 fix un-ignored the first): a buyer never tells his own Hit (`probe_buyer_keeps_his_own_hit_quiet`); a widow whose husband was killed by a stronger stranger, 40 coins and a sharing brother, walks to the Fixer and commissions a Hit on him within 7 days (control: a stronger, braver widow hunts him herself and buys nothing); a lawless suspect buys a Beat on the known witness to his assault (control: an unknown witness is never targeted).
- Hunger as sickness P1 (each failed on d771073 before its fix, `[life] hunger_fixes`): starving with a meal's coins or a coin short, eat before spending or sleeping (G1); lawful and broke beside a stocked Mission, eat there within 6 h (G2); lawless and starving with three meals' coins, buy, never steal (G3); a Beg with no giver is a dry hour, not a failure (G4, mechanism-level).
- Hunger as sickness (`tests/sickness.rs`): a broke hungry adult with no Mission dives a stocked bin within 6 h; a diagnosed patient with the coins is treated at a Clinic within two days.
- Transit (`probe_el_*`, docs/TRANSIT.md § 7, the El on): a commuter with the fare rides and arrives sooner than the walk would, and on time; a broke lawful worker walks; a broke lawless one jumps the gate and rides; a timid worker set out at 22:00 from the worst district never boards (the brave one would); with every line cut, the next workday the worker walks to work.

Gap probes (`#[ignore]`d, for the behaviour round): none open (`probe_buyer_keeps_his_own_hit_quiet`, `docs/GOD_SCENARIOS_V8.md` gap 5, passes since the Hit market's H7).

### Adding a person probe

Copy a probe: build `World::new(42, Config::load())`, run to the hour you need, pick an agent with the helpers (`day_workers_at_work`, the civilian filters), `pin` it, set the stimulus through components (`Needs`, `Wallet`, `Inventory`, `Position`, edges), run with `run_to` (it re-pins every tick and lets you watch each tick), then assert the outcome a person would pick. Read the outcome from the agent's own state (its step, its wallet, its building), never from a city-wide count. If the probe exposes a real gap, mark it `#[ignore]` with the gap in a comment and list it for the behaviour round; do not fix behaviour in the test change.

## God scenarios

The 13 kept, one per reaction class, seed 42, 60 days (the shock at day 45, or day 10/20 in the v5/v6/v7 harnesses), each against its unshocked control: `god_decapitate_gang` (succession), `god_kill_gang` (a gang wiped), `god_city_takeover_by_force` (unlimited resources), `god_bankrupt_city` (the city's money), `god_crackdown_forever` (the law pinned), `god_chrome_everyone_2` (chrome and violence), `god_wipe_zetatech_day_20` (the Virt plane and the tech tree), `god_kill_friend_of_leaders_day_10` (grudges, Hunts, vendettas), `god_export_10x` (the outside world's demand), `god_kill_exec` (corp succession), `god_wipe_corps` (the corps' money), `god_riot_sump_west` (a district riot), `god_evict_sump_rent_10` (housing); M16a's four (v8, `docs/GOD_SCENARIOS_V8.md`): `god_hit_each_exec_day_10` (a Hit on every exec), `god_hit_hollow_leader_from_arasaka` (a corp's Hit on a gang's leader), `god_fixer_licence_off` (the board's lever), `god_law_locate_on_gang_leader` (a public bounty), which assert only that the commands applied; hunger as sickness's one (v9, `docs/GOD_SCENARIOS_V9.md`): `god_famine_synth` (every shelf emptied each morning for 15 days: starvation within the control's + 5, the bins feed more); the Hit market's one (v9): `god_kill_ten_fathers_day_10` (ten household heads killed on day 10, each by a named stranger stronger than his widow: at least one widow or adult child places a Hit within 30 days; the control, the same city unshocked from day 10, prints the same houses; 40 days).
