Sixteen diaries (no arms_dealer diary exists in the folder, so that archetype is unread): forger 29770 and 90429, fixer 100695 and 23959, gun 36512 and 37892, buyer 56165 and 72189, pimp 21245 and 85700, black_doc 61648 and 76794, jailed 19604 and 78427, dodger 28846 and 95969. ## 1. Verdict

Better than V11 in two real ways: walks now mostly arrive (a gun takes a contract, musters, walks 86 tiles, brawls and is arrested inside one day) and jail is no longer a starving void with a 1,171-edge flood (48 MetInJail memories in the worst jailed diary, sleep 8 h, hunger refilled).
Stories still start and rarely get picked up: three of the five "crime" archetypes are not doing their trade (both pimps are plain gang members on an Extort errand, both buyers are corp execs in meetings, the gun with a taken contract plans nothing for a week), and the dodgers are a week of Scavenge for 0c.
No minor is a victim, hit, jailed or dead anywhere in this group; but the 5-11 year-old children in `forger_90429` and `buyer_56165` sit "at home (play)" at 09:30 on all seven days, so S6's School from 6 does not show.

## 5. Dull spots, ranked, with the mechanism

1. **Pimp and buyer as labels.** Mechanism: the archetype's own goal (a pitch round, a Collect, a standing hit) is chosen at spawn when the roster says so, and the gang's Extort errand is a fallback, not the whole week.
2. **A contract that sits.** Mechanism: a taken contract plans a StakeOut at the target's door with a deadline; an expired one posts `ContractLost` and a debt to the poster (`gun_36512`).
3. **Dodgers: a week of Scavenge for 0c.** Mechanism: food comes from a named mark (a stall, a block) so repeat thefts have a victim and a Report; the El ban becomes a walk the dodger takes instead.
4. **The hacker and the clerk-fixer with nothing to serve.** Mechanism: a ledger hack leaves a trace the corp security reads (heat, a fry roll), and a fixer's shop gets the booked walk-ins the camera shop already makes.
5. **Jail's inside is still needs rows only.** Mechanism: a rationed meal, a cellmate's debt or a crew visit as an event line (the "Visited ... an elder parent" line is the model).
6. **The unpaid quit with no grievance walk.** Mechanism: the worker visits the owner for the owed coin and a Confronted line follows (the `fixer_23959` Odile Tanner line is the model).
